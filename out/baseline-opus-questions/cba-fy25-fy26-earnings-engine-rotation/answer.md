# CBA FY25 → FY26: from price to volume, and from release to build

**Verdict: FY26 is the higher-quality year — but hedge earnings, not pricing power, paid for it.**

Sources: **[PA26]** FY26 profit announcement · **[PA25]** FY25 profit announcement · **[P]** FY26 results presentation · **[ASX]** FY26 ASX announcement. All figures cash, continuing operations.

## 1. The rotation

| | FY25 vs FY24 | FY26 vs FY25 |
|---|---|---|
| Operating income | +5% ($28,465m) | +6.2% ($30,224m) |
| Underlying opex | +6% ($12,866m) | +5.6% ($13,585m) |
| Pre-provision profit | **+3%** ($15,469m) | **+6.5%** ($16,469m) |
| Loan impairment expense | $726m, **−9%** | $788m, **+9%** |
| Cash NPAT | +4% ($10,252m) | +7.1% ($10,982m) |
| Headline NIM / AIEA | 2.08%, +9bps / **+1%** | 2.05%, −3bps / **+8%** |
| Cash ROE | 13.5%, **−10bps** | 14.0%, **+50bps** |
| PACC (risk-adjusted) | $5,839m, +5% | $6,464m, +11% |

FY25 was a price year with no balance sheet: NII +5% on a 9bp margin lift and $9bn of AIEA [PA25 p.28]. FY26 inverts it: NII +7% on $92bn of AIEA with margin down 3bps [PA26 p.28].

Arithmetic on the disclosed lines: FY25's $589m pre-tax uplift was $513m of operating performance plus **$76m of impairment relief — 13% of it**. FY26's $938m was **$1,000m of operating performance less $62m of impairment**. FY25 bought 4% with negative jaws and a provision release; FY26 earned 7% with positive jaws while paying more for credit. ROE and PACC settle it: FY25 grew profit while ROE fell; FY26 lifted ROE 50bps and risk-adjusted profit 11%.

## 2. The bridge: compression is optical, stability is hedged

CBA prints two versions of the same 208 → 205bps walk.

| bps | [PA26 p.28] | [P slide 60] |
|---|---|---|
| Liquids (and repos) | (3) | **(4)** — liquids 3, repos 1 |
| Asset pricing | (5) | (5) — home loan 3, rate lag 1, business/insto 1 |
| Funding costs / basis risk | – | – |
| Portfolio mix | +2 | +2 |
| Replicating portfolio + capital hedges | +5 | +5 (RP 4, capital 1) |
| Treasury & Markets | (2) | **(1)** |

They differ only in where the ~1bp institutional-repo drag sits. **I prefer the presentation cut**: it isolates all 4bps of drag from items CBA calls "minimal impact on earnings" (+$15bn liquids, +$11bn repos). Rebased, underlying NIM *rose* 1bp, 2.04% → 2.05% [P slide 6, notes p.130]. So the compression is a mix artefact, not customer-margin erosion.

**The qualification:** underlying NIM held flat only because +5bps of replicating-portfolio and capital-hedge earnings exactly cancelled −5bps of lending price competition. That is a swap-rate outcome, not a franchise one.

## 3. The loss rate is a build, not a bleed

The 8bp rate (from 7bps) sits against *improving* stock: non-performing exposures fell to 0.69% of TCE from 0.73%; net write-offs rose 1bp to 0.07% [PA26 p.45]. FY25 was the opposite — the charge fell 2bps while NPEs rose 14% [PA25 p.45]. CBA raised the Downside scenario weighting [P slide 71] and individually assessed provisions *fell* 3% [PA26 p.44]. Forward-looking build, not realised deterioration.

## 4. What the disclosure does not support

- **Coverage was consumed, not added.** Provisions +2% ($6,476m) against +6% credit RWA, so coverage fell 7bps to 1.53% [PA26 pp.44–45]. Fair on level, not on direction.
- **The second half broke.** 2H26 impairment $469m, +47% (9bps); home-loan arrears 0.73% (+10bps), personal loans 1.72% (+31bps) [PA26 pp.26, 34].
- **Two non-operating helps.** Other income (+$196m) holds an unquantified CommInsure milestone and a Gemini IPO gain [PA26 p.30]; the tax rate fell 50bps to 30.0% [PA26 p.35] — roughly $80m, a tenth of the uplift.
- **Narrow and capital-hungry.** Business Banking +11% and RBS +5% carried it; New Zealand −7%, IB&M +2% [PA26 p.16]. CET1 −30bps to 12.0%, 72bps deployed into credit RWA [ASX p.2].

**Conclusion.** Yes, higher quality: growth came from pre-provision profit rather than the provision line, jaws turned positive, ROE and PACC improved, and the NIM optics are a liquids/repo artefact. Two things stop it being clean — the margin was held by rate hedges rather than pricing, and coverage thinned to fund volume.

**Confidence: 80.**
