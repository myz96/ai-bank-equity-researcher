# Q: Assess whether NAB's FY25 Business & Private Banking growth was value-accretive rather than growth bought at the expense of margin or credit quality. Reconcile lending, revenue and cash-earnings growth with the NIM, impairment-charge and sector/NPL disclosures, and identify what is broad-based versus single-name driven.

*NAB, periods FY25 — confidence 83/100*

## Answer

**Partly value-accretive, but thin.** NAB's B&PB grew GLAs 6.8% and business lending 7.3% in FY25 while cash earnings rose only 1.6% — volume growth was largely absorbed by an 8bp NIM decline, 2.9% cost growth and 9.0% RWA growth, so returns did not improve (cash ROaRWA 2.18% vs 2.19%). Credit quality did **not** deteriorate in the loss line (impairment charge to GLAs 0.19% vs 0.20%), but stock-based asset quality clearly weakened. The 2H25 business-lending NPL step-up is substantially single-name (two large, well-secured Agri customers); the *lending growth* itself is broad-based.

### Reconciling the lines (cash basis, FY25 v FY24 restated)
- Volumes: business lending $166.3bn +7.3%; housing $109.3bn +5.8%; GLAs $279.5bn +6.8% [ev-5, ev-6, ev-7]. AIEA +5.4% [ev-15 context].
- Revenue: NII $7,499m +2.8%; net operating income $8,531m +2.1% [ev-1, ev-2] — i.e. revenue grew at less than a third of GLA growth.
- The wedge is margin: B&PB NIM 3.01% vs 3.09%, **-8bps**, from "competitive pressures impacting both the business and housing lending portfolios, as well as higher deposit costs including mix impacts due to term deposit growth" [ev-8, ev-47, ev-16]. Half-on-half NIM stabilised (2H25 3.02% vs 1H25 3.01%, +1bp; 1H24 3.11%) [ev-39].
- Costs +2.9% (underlying +$102m/+3.2%, largely "investment in additional bankers... to support business growth") lifted CTI to 37.9% from 37.6% [ev-20, ev-14].
- Cash earnings $3,330m, +$53m/+1.6% [ev-4, ev-15]. RWA $159.2bn, +9.0%, from volume growth "combined with deterioration in asset quality" [ev-13, ev-18].

### Credit: flow benign, stock worse
- FY25 charge $529m, +1.1%; ratio 0.19% (FY24 0.20%) [ev-3, ev-11]. But 2H25 $292m vs 1H25 $237m (+23.2%), 0.21% annualised [ev-3, ev-40], driven by "higher business lending charges... higher levels of impairment, asset quality deterioration and volume growth" [ev-19].
- Impaired/GLAs 0.49% (+10bps); default-not-impaired 2.05% (+31bps) [ev-9, ev-10]. B&PB total NPLs $7,079m, 2.54% of GLAs vs 2.12% at Sep 24 [ev-36]. Group NPL rise was "mainly driven by deterioration in the Business and Private Banking business lending portfolio" [ev-21, ev-22].

### Broad-based vs single-name
- **Broad-based:** growth. Agri +3.5%, Health +5.9%, CRE +7.4%, Other +10.4%, small business +9.2% [ev-28]; "Broad based growth across most sectors including Trade, Manufacturing, Construction, Transport" [ev-29]. Australian business lending grew 1.3x system in 2H25 (B&PB+C&IB) [ev-50, ev-52].
- **Single-name:** B&PB business-lending NPL ratio 3.22% (Sep 24 2.66%) "Includes 21bps relating to 2 large Agri customers"; "Excluding two large well secured Agri customers, underlying NPL ratio down 3bps to 3.01%" [ev-33, ev-34, ev-31]. Australian Agri NPLs/EAD 2.95% vs 1.99% at Mar 25, "mainly due to customer specific issues for two large well secured customers" [ev-46, ev-44]. Book 73% fully secured, 6% unsecured [ev-37]; Agri 89% fully secured [ev-45].

### Not settled
No B&PB return-on-equity/RoTE, no B&PB revenue-margin waterfall, no quantified new-business spread, and no dollar size of the two Agri names.

## Limitations
- No B&PB return-on-equity or RoTE is disclosed in either document, so 'value-accretive' can only be tested via cash earnings on average RWA (2.18% vs 2.19%) and cash earnings on average assets (1.25% vs 1.30%).
- Neither document prints a B&PB-level NIM waterfall or a new-business lending spread, so the split of the 8bp NIM fall between front-book price competition, deposit cost and mix is not quantified.
- The dollar exposure of the two large Agri customers is not disclosed; only the 21bps NPL-ratio impact is printed.
- FY24 comparatives for B&PB are restated twice (portfolio transfers between Personal Banking and B&PB, and a change in expense presentation), so all growth rates are on restated bases.
- B&PB credit impairment charge and NPE figures on presentation p.95 cover the total B&PB portfolio including mortgages, so they are not a pure business-lending read.
- Presentation p.8 volume-growth and 1.3x-system figures combine B&PB and C&IB Australian business lending; they are not B&PB-only.
- B&PB business-lending sector NPL levels are shown as a chart of bars without printed values for each sector, so only the aggregate ratios could be quoted.
