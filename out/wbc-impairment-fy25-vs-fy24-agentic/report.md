The impairment note's provision-type rows bridge the movement exactly: write-backs -102, recoveries -57, new IAPs -15, other changes in CAPs -14, offset by higher CAP write-offs +75 (ev-2 to ev-7). The pack's FY24-FY25 net profit walk independently prints an impairment charges bar of +$113m to profit (ev-30). Net write-offs to average gross loans rose 5bps to 6bps (ev-19).

- **Divisional comparability FY24 vs FY25** (restatement): FY24 segment comparatives not revised vs 1H25 restated for segment composition changes
  Preferred: Provision-type bridge (p21) for quantification. The bank states "1H25 has been restated for the above changes. Divisional impairment deltas therefore mix real credit movements with composition shifts, so the where-layer is narrative only and the quantified bridge is taken from the provision-type rows.

## Limitations
- The divisional where-layer deltas are computed by me from the two period columns (FY25 less FY24, ev-20/ev-21): Consumer -31 (217 vs 248), Business & Wealth +103 (245 vs 142), Institutional -121 (benefit of 1 vs charge of 120), New Zealand -66 (benefit of 41 vs charge of 25), Group Businesses +2 (4 vs 2); these sum to -113. The bank does not print a divisional change column for impairment, and FY24 divisional comparatives are not restated for FY25 segment composition changes (ev-22), so these divisional deltas are less reliable than the provision-type bridge.
- The bank publishes no FY24-to-FY25 waterfall of the impairment charge itself. The pack's IMPAIRMENT CHARGES COMPOSITION chart (p49) is on a 2H24/1H25/2H25 half basis and is context only; the only primary-comparison chart is the net profit walk (ev-30), which prints impairment as a single +$113m bar with no further decomposition.
- 'Other changes in CAPs' (-$14m) is a net bar spanning opposing forces the bank names but does not individually quantify in the charge: improved commercial property price and interest rate outlook and lower mortgage delinquencies against a 5ppt downside scenario weight increase and new portfolio overlays (ev-12 to ev-15). The $168m modelled CAP decrease and $59m overlay increase (ev-37, ev-36) are provision-BALANCE movements, not components of the period charge.
- The collective-provision movement cannot be split between portfolio volume growth and risk migration from the disclosure available; no evidence supports a collective.volume or collective.asset_quality contribution, so neither is claimed.
- The loss rate denominators differ slightly by page: p21 prints 'Impairment charges/(benefits) to average loans' (5bps vs 7bps, ev-9) while p9 says '5 basis points of average gross loans' (ev-42); the pack labels it 'Impairment charges to average loans annualised' (ev-35). All give the same 2bps improvement.
- Basis normalised from 'cash' to 'ex_notables': no page in evidence prints 'cash' beside the movement, and the registry names ex_notables as the bank's headline basis.
- Capped at 80: write_backs_recoveries -102 $m, write_backs_recoveries -57 $m, individual_provisions -15 $m, write_offs_direct +75 $m, overlays_fla -14 $m. The records these claims cite do not state those numbers, so each one is arithmetic over the evidence rather than a figure read from it.
