# CBA — cet1 — FY21 vs FY20

**Movement (cash basis):** 1160bps → 1310bps (+150bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 92/100

*Read from: row 'Common Equity Tier 1 (APRA Level 2) ratio', column 30 Jun 20 column -> column 30 Jun 21 column*

CBA's APRA Level 2 (Group) CET1 ratio rose 150 bpts in FY21, from 11.6% at 30 June 2020 to 13.1% at 30 June 2021 (ev-12, ev-13, ev-17, ev-19). The full-year walk (presentation p111, sum check passed) attributes the +150 bpts to: Divestments +42, APRA Operational Risk add-on reduction +17, 2H20 dividend -32, Cash NPAT +88, Underlying RWA -13, Other -2 (1H21); then 1H21 dividend -59, Cash NPAT +97, RWA +8, Other +4 (2H21). The bank's own half-on-half narrative for the 6 months to Jun 21 states capital generated from earnings +97 bpts, lower total RWA +8 bpts, other regulatory adjustments +4 bpts, partly offset by the 2021 interim dividend -59 bpts (ev-14). CET1 remained above APRA's 'unquestionably strong' 10.5% benchmark throughout FY21 (ev-18).
