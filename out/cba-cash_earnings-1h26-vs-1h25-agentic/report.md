## Limitations
- The bank publishes no single cash-NPAT waterfall in dollars for 1H26 vs 1H25; the bridge here is assembled from the Group Performance Summary rows plus each component note's own stated 'on the prior comparative period' movement. The components sum exactly to the $313m movement, leaving zero residual.
- NII is claimed as a single $761m total. CBA does not publish a dollar split of NII into volume and margin effects, so nii.volume and nii.margin carry no contribution — only the AIEA (+$96bn, +8%) and NIM (-4bps) statements and the bps NIM walk.
- The NIM walk bars are in basis points, not dollars, and cannot be converted to a dollar contribution; they explain the margin leg inside the $761m NII figure and must not be added to the dollar bridge.
- CBA qualifies the liquids leg: growth in liquid assets has 'broadly neutral impacts on net interest income', so the -3bps liquids bar and the -4bps headline NIM overstate the economic margin pressure; the underlying NIM excluding liquids and institutional repos was unchanged, down just 1bp on the reported measure.
- The underlying opex walk bars (inflation +275, technology +222, frontline +78, other -6, productivity -221) are gross legs inside the net $348m: the $221m productivity benefit offsets much of the gross investment, and the bank repeatedly notes increases were 'partly offset by productivity initiatives'.
- Operating expenses are stated on the UNDERLYING basis ($6,720m, +$348m, excluding notable items); the headline total opex movement was +$518m (+8%) to $6,890m once the $170m of notable items is included.
- The tax contribution is a mechanical consequence of higher pre-tax profit rather than an independent driver; the effective tax rate actually fell 40bpts to 30.3%, a modest favourable offset the bridge does not isolate.
- Divisional cash NPAT movements (Business Banking +14% to $2,272m, RBS +1% to $2,727m) are not reconciled to the Group component bridge here; no divisional attribution is claimed.
- Comparative information has been restated to conform to current-period presentation, per the Group Performance Summary footnote.
- Expenses are claimed on the underlying/notable split; the bank equally publishes the combined headline framing, so both claims are capped at 80.
