# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 92/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column 30 Jun 25 -> column 30 Jun 26*

CBA's Group net interest margin (cash basis) decreased 3 basis points year-on-year, from 2.08% in FY25 to 2.05% in FY26. The decline was driven by 5 bps of headwind from asset pricing (home and business/institutional lending competition), partially offset by 5 bps tailwind from capital and replicating portfolio earnings, 2 bps from favourable portfolio mix (business lending and deposit growth), and 3 bps drag from growth in liquid assets. Funding costs and basis risk were flat. Excluding liquid assets, underlying margin was stable.
