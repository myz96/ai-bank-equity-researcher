# CBA — cet1 — 1H26 vs 1H25

**Movement (cash basis):** 1220bps → 1230bps (+10bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 40/100

*Read from: row 'Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) APRA Level 2', column 31 Dec 2024 column -> column 31 Dec 2025 column*

CBA's APRA Level 2 CET1 ratio increased 10 bps to 12.3% at 31 December 2025 from 12.2% at 31 December 2024. The movement was driven by capital generated from earnings (cash NPAT rose 6% to $5,445 million), partly offset by the payment of the 2025 final dividend (including a DRP on-market purchase with CET1 impact of -13 bps) and higher total RWA ($505.3 billion, up $22.9 billion or 5% from $482.4 billion). Higher Credit Risk, Traded Market Risk and Operational Risk RWA were partly offset by lower IRRBB RWA which includes the impact of adoption of the revised APS 117 framework (CET1 impact of +24 bps). Other regulatory adjustments and movement in reserves also contributed. The International CET1 ratio declined 50 bps to 18.3%.
