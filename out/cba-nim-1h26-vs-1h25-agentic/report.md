# CBA — nim — 1H26 vs 1H25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 204bps (-4bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 93/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column 31 Dec 24 -> column 31 Dec 25*

CBA's Group net interest margin fell 4bps to 2.04% in 1H26 from 2.08% in 1H25 (KPI table and Net Interest Income table, cash basis) (ev-2, ev-10). Net interest income still rose $761m or 6% to $12,695m, driven by an 8% ($96bn) rise in average interest earning assets to $1,232bn, partly offset by the 4bp margin decline (ev-8). The results book walk attributes the fall to Liquids -3, Asset pricing -2, Funding costs -3, Portfolio mix +1, Basis risk 0, Capital/Replicating & Other +6 and Treasury & Markets -3 (ev-1). Excluding liquids growth, which the Bank calls broadly neutral for net interest income, NIM fell just 1bp (ev-3); the highlights page, excluding both liquids and institutional repos, calls underlying NIM "flat" on 1H25 (ev-25). The presentation's 12-month walk agrees on the endpoints and on asset pricing, funding and mix, but groups repos with liquids (-4) and shows Treasury & Markets at -2 (ev-13).

## Source disagreements
- **Grouping of institutional repos and Treasury & Markets in the NIM walk** (definitional): Book: Liquids -3, Treasury & Markets -3 vs Slide: Liquids & repos -4, Treasury & Markets -2
  Preferred: Book: Liquids -3, Treasury & Markets -3. Both walks start at 208 and end at 204 and both sum. The presentation moves the ~1bp drag from higher institutional reverse repo balances out of Treasury & Markets and into the liquids bar (slide annotation: increase in institutional repos -1). Per the walk-preference rule the results book's framing is primary.
- **Underlying / ex-volatile NIM movement on 1H25** (definitional): -1bp excluding liquids growth vs flat on underlying basis (excl. liquids and institutional repos)
  Preferred: -1bp excluding liquids growth. Page 28 of the results book excludes only growth in liquid assets and reports NIM down 1bp; the highlights page also excludes institutional reverse repos and calls underlying NIM flat on 1H25. Different exclusion sets, not a numerical conflict. Neither is the headline measure, which is 2.04% (-4bps).

## Limitations
- The liquids drag and part of the Treasury & Markets drag are dilution rather than earnings loss: the Bank calls liquid asset growth "broadly neutral" for net interest income (ev-3) and the Carbon and Commodities / Global Markets funding cost is "offset in other operating income" (ev-24). The 4bp headline decline therefore overstates the underlying margin compression, which the Bank puts at -1bp (excl. liquids) or flat (excl. liquids and repos).
- The funding costs bar (-3bps) is not split between deposit pricing and wholesale funding, so it is mapped to the parent 'funding' node.
- 'Capital, Replicating & Other' is a composite bar; only the replicating portfolio (+5) and capital hedges (+1) are quantified, with no figure given for 'Other'.
- The Group NIM row carries no cash/statutory label; it is reported on CBA's primary cash basis per the KPI page footnote (ev-12).
