# Analyst note

**Judgment: FY26's faster cash-NPAT growth was higher quality.** The engine rotated from pre-provision growth plus a credit-cost release to volume-led revenue and operating leverage absorbing worse impairments.

**NIM reconciliation.** Reported NIM fell 3bp, 2.08% to 2.05%. CBA's bridge shows -4bp liquids/repos, -5bp asset pricing, +5bp hedging, +2bp mix, -1bp Treasury & Markets, and flat funding; it calls the liquid/repo drag “minimal impact on earnings.” *(CBA-FY26-results-presentation.pdf, PDF p. 60, “208 205 - 2 5 (4) (5) (1) FY25 Liquids & repos Asset pricing Funding costs Portfolio mix Interest rate risk hedging Treasury & Markets FY26” and “Minimal impact on earnings”)* The profit announcement assigns -3bp to liquids and -2bp to Treasury & Markets because the latter includes repo growth; both reach 2.05%. *(CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 28, “decreased by 3 basis points on the prior year to 2.05%”; PDF p. 29, “Decreased margin by 2 basis points due to lower risk management income in Treasury and growth in reverse sale and repurchase agreement balances.”)*

FY25's reported +9bp NIM was not categorically better: +7bp came from lower liquids/pooled facilities, leaving only +2bp underlying. *(CBA-FY25-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 28, “increased 9 basis points on the prior year to 2.08%” and “Excluding a 7 basis point increase in margin from a reduction in lower yielding liquid assets and institutional pooled facilities” and “net interest margin increased 2 basis points.”)*

Thus FY26 was higher quality: profit accelerated despite a credit headwind—not from margin expansion. Limits: unquantified OOI one-offs; management-attributed bridge; 8bp is not a through-cycle or realised-loss forecast. Confidence: **93/100**.
