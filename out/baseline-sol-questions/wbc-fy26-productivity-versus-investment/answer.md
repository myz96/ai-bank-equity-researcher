# Westpac FY26 productivity versus investment pressure

## Judgment

**No: the disclosure does not support underwriting positive jaws in FY26. The productivity target looks mainly like an offset to continuing cost pressure, rather than a net cost-out promise.** Positive jaws remain possible, but Westpac gives neither an FY26 income-growth forecast nor a total expense-growth target.

FY25 is the warning. Net operating income grew 3% (WBC-FY25-results-announcement.pdf, PDF page 9, “Net operating income 22,464 21,763 3”), while operating expenses grew 9% (WBC-FY25-results-announcement.pdf, PDF page 9, “Operating expenses (11,916) (10,944) 9”)—or 6% excluding the $273m restructuring charge—so jaws were clearly negative. (WBC-FY25-results-announcement.pdf, PDF page 9, “Excluding these costs, operating expenses increased 6% mainly due to higher staff costs and the step up in UNITE investment spend.”)

The FY25 bridge shows $402m of productivity against $397m staff, $146m technology, $199m volume/other and $359m investment pressure, before a $273m restructuring charge; expenses rose from $10,944m to $11,916m. (WBC-FY25-presentation-and-IDP.pdf, PDF page 27, “10,944 397 146 199 359 273 11,916 (402)”) (WBC-FY25-presentation-and-IDP.pdf, PDF page 27, “Staff costs Technology Volume and other Productivity Investments Restructuring charge”) Productivity absorbed only part of the pressure.

Westpac targets >$500m productivity (WBC-FY25-presentation-and-IDP.pdf, PDF page 27, “Fit for Growth benefits to contribute to targeted productivity of >$500m in FY26”), but flags a 3%-to-4% EBA increase and banker investment (WBC-FY25-presentation-and-IDP.pdf, PDF page 27, “EBA increase 3% to 4% and investment in bankers”), plus higher UNITE spend. (WBC-FY25-presentation-and-IDP.pdf, PDF page 27, “Increase in UNITE spend”)

The investment mix reinforces the pressure. FY25 investment was $1,918m (WBC-FY25-results-announcement.pdf, PDF page 19, “Total investment spend of $1,918 million was 9% higher”): 34% UNITE, 30% growth/productivity and 36% risk/regulatory. (WBC-FY25-results-announcement.pdf, PDF page 19, “UNITE accounted for 34%, growth and productivity initiatives accounted for 30% and 36% was directed towards risk and regulatory activities.”) UNITE itself was $660m and 74% expensed. (WBC-FY25-results-announcement.pdf, PDF page 19, “UNITE investment spend increased to $660 million with 74% expensed.”)

In FY26, UNITE rises to $850m-$950m (WBC-FY25-presentation-and-IDP.pdf, PDF page 14, “Expect to invest $850 - $950 million in FY26”) with about 75% expensed. (WBC-FY25-presentation-and-IDP.pdf, PDF page 14, “c.75% of spend to be expensed”) Total investment stays around $2bn (WBC-FY25-presentation-and-IDP.pdf, PDF page 26, “Investment spend ~$2bn”), with other categories falling. (WBC-FY25-presentation-and-IDP.pdf, PDF page 26, “Expect reductions in both growth & productivity and risk & regulation spend”) The program remains heavy through FY27-FY28 (WBC-FY25-presentation-and-IDP.pdf, PDF page 14, “c.40% of total investment spend1 FY27 – FY28”) and extends into FY29. (WBC-FY25-presentation-and-IDP.pdf, PDF page 14, “Plan extends into FY29”)

## Limitations

Westpac does not disclose FY26 revenue growth, total expense growth, the dollar reduction in non-UNITE investment, the split between gross and recurring productivity, or a UNITE-specific amortisation period. The disclosed 2.7-year average applies to all capitalised software, not UNITE alone. (WBC-FY25-results-announcement.pdf, PDF page 20, “Average amortisation period (years) 2.7 3.1 (0.4) years”.)

**Confidence: 92/100**
