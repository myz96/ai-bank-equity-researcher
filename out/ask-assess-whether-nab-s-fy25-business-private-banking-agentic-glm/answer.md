# Q: Assess whether NAB's FY25 Business & Private Banking growth was value-accretive rather than growth bought at the expense of margin or credit quality. Reconcile lending, revenue and cash-earnings growth with the NIM, impairment-charge and sector/NPL disclosures, and identify what is broad-based versus single-name driven.

*NAB, periods FY25 — confidence 88/100*

**Verdict: broadly value-preserving rather than clearly value-accretive.** FY25 B&PB cash earnings rose 1.6% to $3,330m on revenue up only 2.1%, while business lending grew 7.3% and deposits 6.6% — volume was partly bought with margin (NIM −8bps to 3.01%) and returns per dollar of balance sheet slipped. Credit quality deteriorated, but the P&L charge did not (charge/GLAs −1bp to 0.19%), and the NPL rise is largely two well-secured Agri names.

**Reconciliation.** Lending→revenue: business lending $155.0bn→$166.3bn (+7.3%), housing $103.3bn→$109.3bn (+5.8%), deposits $223.1bn→$237.9bn (+6.6%); NII +2.8% to $7,499m; other operating income −2.9% (underlying fees −$9m/−0.9%). Revenue lagged volumes because NIM fell 8bps on "competitive pressures impacting both the business and housing lending portfolios, as well as higher deposit costs including mix impacts due to term deposit growth". At group level, lending margin fell only 1bp, with "favourable portfolio mix from stronger higher yielding business lending growth" — the business growth is mix-accretive to the group. Revenue→cash earnings: expenses +2.9% (underlying +$102m/3.2% on extra bankers and technology) lifted cost-to-income 30bps to 37.9%; impairment +$6m; cash earnings +$53m (+1.6%). Returns: cash earnings/average assets 1.25% (−5bps), /average RWA 2.18% (−1bp), with RWA +9.0% — partly "deterioration in asset quality".

**Credit quality.** Divisional impaired assets/GLAs +10bps to 0.49% and default-but-not-impaired +31bps to 2.05%; group NPE +18.2% ($1,864m) to $12,094m (1.55% of GLAs, +16bps), "mainly driven by deterioration in the Business and Private Banking business lending portfolio"; individually assessed provisions +53.8% to $1,163m driven by B&PB business lending. Yet the divisional charge/GLAs improved 1bp to 0.19% (2H25 0.21% vs 2H24 0.25%), helped by collective releases (group: $215m target-sector FLA release; collective/CRWA 1.33%, −14bps).

**Broad-based vs single-name.** Growth is broad-based: "growth in business lending across a broad range of sectors"; segment growth Agri +3.5%, Health +5.9%, CRE +7.4%, Other +10.4%; SME share 21.7%. Deterioration is substantially single-name: two large well-secured Agri customers contribute 21bps of the 3.22% B&PB business-lending NPL ratio; excluding them it fell 3bps to 3.01%, with "stable to improving NPL outcomes in 2H25 for most sectors".

**Not settled:** the Agri exposures' dollar size; any B&PB NPE dollar split; the price-vs-mix split of the 8bps NIM decline.
