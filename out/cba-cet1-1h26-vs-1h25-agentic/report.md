- **CET1 measure variant: internationally comparable ratio moves the opposite way** (definitional): APRA Level 2: 12.2% -> 12.3%, +10 bpts vs Internationally comparable: 18.8% -> 18.3%, -50 bpts
  Preferred: APRA Level 2: 12.2% -> 12.3%, +10 bpts. The internationally comparable CET1 ratio applies Basel-framework adjustments to APRA's more conservative framework and is a different measure; the task's headline measure is the APRA Level 2 (Group) ratio from the results book KPI table (ev-24, ev-17).
- **Level 1 vs Level 2 CET1 ratio** (definitional): Level 2: 12.2% -> 12.3% vs Level 1: 12.1% -> 12.2%
  Preferred: Level 2: 12.2% -> 12.3%. Level 1 is the parent-bank consolidation, a different measure quoted as context only (ev-16).

## Limitations
- CBA publishes no CET1 walk for the task comparison (Dec 24 -> Dec 25). Both the results book (p.28) and the slides (pp.33, 103) show only the Jun 25 -> Dec 25 half-on-half bridge, which read_chart classified as context. Its bars (Cash NPAT +107, 2H25 dividend -87, RWA -33, Other +9) are therefore described inside narratives, never used as contributions.
- Consequently only the two footnoted, period-matching events are quantified (+24 bpts APS 117; -13 bpts DRP on-market purchase). These do not, and are not intended to, sum to the +10 bpts movement; the residual of roughly -1 bpt is nominal and the true unexplained portion is essentially the whole movement.
- The -13 bpts dividend figure covers only the DRP neutralisation of the 2025 final dividend, not the gross dividend paid; the 1H25 interim dividend paid during 2H25 (also DRP-neutralised, 18.1% participation) sits inside the 12-month window but its bpts impact is not separately disclosed.
- The read_chart sum check on the half-on-half walk FAILED (1230 + (-4) = 1226 vs printed end 1230), consistent with the bank's own note that "numbers presented in this section may not sum precisely to the totals provided" due to rounding.
- The bank states IRRBB's APS 117 benefit was "partly offset by the impact of higher long-term interest rates in Australia reducing embedded gains", so the +24 bpts gross benefit exceeds the net IRRBB contribution (+11 bpts half-on-half).
- The Cash NPAT walk bar excludes equity accounted profits/losses and impairments from associates (capital neutral) and the Credit RWA bar excludes FX, which is carried in 'Other' - so those bars are not comparable to the reported P&L or RWA totals.
- No divestment or acquisition CET1 bpts impact is disclosed for the window; no such bar appears in the published walk.
- RWA figures in the corpus quotes are printed in $M/$bn; the $m unit tag on those evidence numbers reflects the tool's unit vocabulary, not a restatement.
- Capped at 80: dividend_net_drp -13 bps. Each of these claims cites a walk whose own bars do not sum to that chart's endpoints, so the read it rests on disagrees with itself.
