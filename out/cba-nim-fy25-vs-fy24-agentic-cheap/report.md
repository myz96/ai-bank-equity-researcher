# CBA — nim — FY25 vs FY24

**Movement (cash basis):** 199bps → 208bps (+9bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column 30 Jun 24 -> column 30 Jun 25*

CBA's net interest margin increased 9 bps year-on-year to 2.08% in FY25 (from 1.99% in FY24). Excluding a 7 bps benefit from reduced liquid assets and institutional pooled facilities (which have a broadly neutral impact on NII), underlying NIM improved only 2 bps. The improvement was primarily driven by +9 bps from capital and replicating portfolio hedges (replicating portfolio up 5 bps, capital hedges up 4 bps) and +1 bps from Treasury and Markets, largely offset by −7 bps from funding costs (increased deposit price competition) and −1 bps from adverse basis risk. Asset pricing and portfolio mix were flat.
