# CBA — nim — FY25 vs FY24

**Movement (cash basis):** 199bps → 208bps (+9bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column Full Year Ended 30 Jun 24 -> column Full Year Ended 30 Jun 25*

CBA's net interest margin increased 9 bps year-on-year to 2.08% (from 1.99% in FY24). Excluding a 7 bps benefit from reduced liquid assets and institutional pooled facilities, underlying NIM improved only 2 bps. The increase was primarily driven by +9 bps from capital, replicating portfolio and hedge earnings (replicating portfolio +5 bps, capital hedges +4 bps), partially offset by −7 bps from funding costs (increased deposit price competition) and −1 bps from adverse basis risk (wider bank bill/OIS spread). Treasury and Markets contributed +1 bps. Asset pricing and portfolio mix were flat.
