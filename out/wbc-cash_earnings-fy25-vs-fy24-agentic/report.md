## Limitations
- The bank publishes no cash earnings measure; net profit excluding Notable Items is treated as cash-comparable and is a non-AAS, unaudited, unreviewed measure by Westpac's own statement (ev-1 page text).
- The adopted walk (IDP p40) bars are after-tax-equivalent only in aggregate: NII, non-interest income, expenses and impairment bars are pre-tax movements and the tax effect sits in the single 'Tax & NCI' bar, so the components are not individually tax-adjusted contributions to net profit.
- Volume and margin within NII are not quantified in dollars by the bank; only the +557 total bar, AIEA growth (3%) and NIM (-1bp) are disclosed, so nii.volume and nii.margin cannot be split in $m.
- The expenses bar is a net number: Westpac reports $402m of productivity savings offsetting gross increases (staff +$397m, investments +$359m, volume and other +$199m, technology +$146m), and says productivity 'provided a partial offset'. The -$972m headline therefore understates gross cost pressure.
- The $273m restructuring charge is disclosed as a sub-part of the expenses bar, not as a separate walk bar, so it must not be added to the -$972m; showing it as a separate quantified component risks double counting and it is flagged as such.
- The impairment bar of +113 is a net movement: a swing to a $34m IAP benefit from a $140m charge was partly offset by a higher CAP charge ($458m vs $397m) including an increase for new portfolio overlays and a 5 percentage point rise in the downside scenario weight.
- The results announcement does not publish its own FY24-FY25 profit bridge chart; the adopted walk is the slide/IDP chart (p40), corroborated component-by-component by results-book tables on pages 9, 11, 16, 18 and 21.
- Basis normalised from 'cash' to 'ex_notables': no page in evidence prints 'cash' beside the movement, and the registry names ex_notables as the bank's headline basis.
- Expenses are claimed on the underlying/notable split; the bank equally publishes the combined headline framing, so both claims are capped at 80.
