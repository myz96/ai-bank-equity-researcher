# Q: How did CBA's earnings engine rotate from FY25 to FY26, and was FY26's faster cash-NPAT growth higher quality despite reported NIM compression and a higher loan-loss rate? Reconcile the two years' income/expense/impairment mix with CBA's own FY26 margin bridge.

*CBA, periods FY26, FY25 — confidence 88/100*

**Direct answer.** CBA's earnings engine rotated from margin-led to volume-led. In FY25 NII rose 5% on a 9bps NIM gain (2.08% vs 1.99%) against only 1% AIEA growth; in FY26 NII rose 7% on 8% AIEA growth ($1,245,684m vs $1,153,684m) while NIM fell 3bps to 2.05%. FY26's faster cash-NPAT growth (7.1% to $10,982m vs 4% to $10,252m) was higher quality on CBA's own mix — positive jaws (income +6.2% vs underlying opex +5.6%, after FY25's negative jaws of +5% vs +6%), cost-to-income down to 45.5% from 45.7%, ROE +50bps to 14.0% — with two caveats: the reported NIM compression is largely a liquids/repo mix effect, and FY26 income included one-off gains while the loan-loss rate rose.

**Two-year mix (cash basis, continuing operations).** FY25: income $28,465m (+5%); underlying opex $12,866m (+6%); notables $130m; total opex $12,996m; operating performance $15,469m (+3%); LIE $726m (−9%); cash NPAT $10,252m (+4%). FY26: income $30,224m (+6.2%); underlying opex $13,585m (+5.6%); notables $170m; total opex $13,755m (+6%); operating performance $16,469m (+6.5%); LIE $788m (+8.5%); cash NPAT $10,982m (+7.1%). Statutory NPAT (continuing) rose 8% to $10,911m.

**FY26 margin bridge (CBA's own, 2.08%→2.05%).** Liquids −3bps (presentation: −4bps "Liquids & repos", split −3 liquids/−1 institutional repos, "minimal impact on earnings"; +$15bn liquids, +$11bn repos); asset pricing −5bps (home-loan competition −3, cash-rate lag −1, business/insto −1); funding costs flat; portfolio mix +2bps; hedging +5bps (replicating +4, capital +1); Treasury & Markets −2bps per the profit announcement (presentation shows −1bps under its grouping). Ex-liquid assets, NIM "was stable". FY25's +9bps was flattered by +7bps from lower liquids/pooled facilities and +9bps capital/replicating, against funding costs −7bps; underlying +2bps.

**Quality caveats.** Other income +$46m (+42%) to $156m incl. the Gemini fair-value gain and CommInsure milestone; associates/equity investments swung to +$133m from −$145m (FY25 incl. $222m Bank of Hangzhou loss). LIE/GLAA rose to 8bps (FY25: 7bps); collective provision funding $606m vs $456m; home-loan 90+ arrears 0.73% (+10bps); 2H26 LIE +47% on 1H26; provisions/credit RWA 1.53% vs 1.60%. Offsets: individual write-backs $240m vs $169m and lower individual provisioning ($422m vs $439m).

**Not settled:** whether the +5bps hedging tailwind repeats in FY27; the FY25 LIE segment comparative is "restated to conform to presentation" (group totals unchanged); the 4 Aug 2026 note says home-loan arrears methodology changes did not affect 90+ day arrears, provisioning or capital.

## Limitations
- 'Higher quality' is an analytical judgement on mix (jaws, cost-to-income, ROE, volume-led NII, one-off income content); CBA does not publish a defined earnings-quality metric.
- The FY26 presentation (slide 60) and profit announcement (p13) group the NIM bridge differently: the presentation shows 'Interest rate risk hedging +5bps' and 'Treasury & Markets −1bps', the profit announcement shows 'Capital, Replicating and Other +5bps' and 'Treasury and Markets −2bps'; both reconcile to −3bps overall.
- The FY25 comparative in the FY26 loan impairment segment table is 'restated to conform to presentation in the current period' (segment level; group LIE totals agree at $726m).
- The FY25 NIM bridge is read off the FY25 profit announcement chart (p28); its bar labels/values were machine-read and the printed narrative confirms only the +9bps total, the +7bps liquids/pooled effect and the +2bps underlying move.
- The note does not quantify FY27 outlook beyond what was read; whether the replicating-portfolio/capital-hedge tailwind (+5bps) repeats is not established by the documents read.
- Other operating income one-offs (Gemini fair-value gain, CommInsure milestone) are described but not separately dollar-quantified by CBA on the pages read.
- Research stopped early: the wall-clock budget (1800s) was reached, so the evidence behind this answer is less complete than a full run's.
