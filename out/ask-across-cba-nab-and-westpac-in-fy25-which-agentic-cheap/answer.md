**Comparability limits:**
The three banks' preferred measures are not identical. CBA reports "cash NPAT" which excludes hedging/IFRS volatility and acquisition/disposal gains/losses. NAB reports "cash earnings" with similar but not identical adjustments (page 75 reconciliation). Westpac reports "net profit excluding Notable Items" — not a cash measure since 1H23 — which excludes five defined categories of Notable Items (primarily hedging). The measures differ in scope of excluded fair-value items, meaning direct comparison of absolute levels is limited even when conversion ratios are comparable.

## Limitations
- The three banks' preferred earnings measures (cash NPAT, cash earnings, net profit excl. Notable Items) are not identical — they differ in what fair-value/hedging items are excluded, limiting direct comparability of absolute levels.
- Pre-provision profit definitions also differ slightly: CBA uses 'operating performance', NAB uses 'profit before credit impairment charge' on cash basis, and Westpac uses 'pre-provision profit excl. Notable Items'.
