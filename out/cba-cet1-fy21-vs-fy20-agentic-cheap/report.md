# CBA — cet1 — FY21 vs FY20

**Movement (cash basis):** 1160bps → 1310bps (+150bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

*Read from: row 'Common Equity Tier 1 (APRA Level 2)', column 30 Jun 20 column -> column 30 Jun 21 column*

CBA's APRA Level 2 CET1 ratio rose 150 bps to 13.1% in FY21 (from 11.6% in FY20), driven by strong organic capital generation from earnings (+185 bps across both halves), divestment proceeds (+42 bps), and an APRA operational risk add-on reduction (+17 bps), partially offset by dividends paid (-91 bps) and a modest net RWA increase (-5 bps). The internationally comparable CET1 ratio was 19.4% (up 200 bps from 17.4%).

## Limitations
- The walk chart on page 111 of the results presentation is the primary source for the full-year decomposition. The profit announcement (page 53) only discloses half-on-half drivers for 1H21, not the full-year breakdown.
- The bank does not separately quantify the full-year earnings generation driver; the +185 bps figure is derived by summing the 2H20 (+88 bps) and 1H21 (+97 bps) Cash NPAT bars from the walk chart.
- No off-market buy-back occurred in FY21; the $6 billion buy-back mentioned on page 13 was announced but not executed within the period.
- Capped at 80: earnings_generation +185 bps, dividend_net_drp -91 bps, rwa -5 bps. The records these claims cite do not state those numbers, so each one is arithmetic over the evidence rather than a figure read from it.
