# CBA — cet1 — FY21 vs FY20

**Movement (cash basis):** 1160bps → 1310bps (+150bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 85/100

*Read from: row 'Common Equity Tier 1 (APRA Level 2 Group)', column 30 Jun 20 column -> column 30 Jun 21 column*

CBA's APRA Level 2 CET1 ratio rose 150 bpts to 13.1% in FY21 (from 11.6% in FY20), well above APRA's 10.5% 'unquestionably strong' benchmark. The improvement was driven by $8.7 billion of cash NPAT (+185 bpts total across both halves), divestment proceeds (+42 bpts from BoCommLife, CommInsure Life and other sales), and an APRA-operational-risk-add-on reduction (+17 bpts per APRA's Nov 2020 announcement cutting the add-on 50% from $12.5 bn to $6.25 bn), partly offset by dividends paid (-91 bpts; 2020 final DRP issued shares while 2021 interim DRP was fully on-market purchased at $418 m / 9 bpts impact), and net RWA movement (-5 bpts; credit RWA up $7.4 bn on volume growth, offset by $11.0 bn OpRisk RWA decline). The internationally comparable CET1 ratio rose 200 bpts to 19.4%.
