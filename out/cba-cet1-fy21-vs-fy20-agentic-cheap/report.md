# CBA — cet1 — FY21 vs FY20

**Movement (cash basis):** 1160bps → 1310bps (+150bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

*Read from: row 'Common Equity Tier 1 (APRA Level 2)', column 30 Jun 20 column -> column 30 Jun 21 column*

CBA's APRA Level 2 CET1 ratio rose 150 bps to 13.1% in FY21 vs 11.6% in FY20, driven by strong organic capital generation from cash NPAT (+185 bps across both halves), divestment proceeds (+42 bps from BoCommLife, CommInsure Life and other sales), and an APRA operational risk add-on reduction (+17 bps). These were partly offset by dividends paid (-91 bps total, including a DRP-neutralised interim dividend via on-market share purchase) and a modest net RWA headwind (-5 bps). The internationally comparable CET1 ratio was 19.4% (+200 bps).
