### asset_pricing — "Asset pricing"
*-2 bps | confidence 90/100*

Decreased margin by 2 basis points: lower business lending margins (down 1 bpt) from repricing actions to support businesses during COVID-19, and lower consumer finance margins (down 1 bpt) from a reduction in the proportion of interest earning credit card balances. Home lending margins were flat, reflecting repricing (up 9 bpts), offset by switching to lower margin loans, particularly variable to fixed rate (down 5 bpts) and increased competition (down 4 bpts) (ev-18).
> [ev-18] CBA/FY21/profit_announcement, printed p12: "Asset pricing: Decreased margin by 2 basis points driven by lower business lending margins (down 1 basis point) from repricing actions to support businesses during COVID-19, and lower consumer finance margins (down 1 basis point) from a reduction in the proportion of interest earning credit card balances. Home lending margins were flat, reflecting repricing (up 9 basis points), offset by the impact of customers switching from higher margin loans to lower margin loans, particularly from variable rate to fixed rate loans (down 5 basis points) and increased competition (down 4 basis points)."

### basis_risk — "Basis risk"
*+3 bps | confidence 90/100*

Basis risk arises from the spread between the 3 month bank bill swap rate and the 3 month overnight index swap rate. The Bank's margin increased 3 basis points reflecting a decrease in the average spread notwithstanding a structural reduction in exposure to basis risk due to strong growth in cash rate linked deposits and a mix shift towards fixed rate home loans (ev-21).
