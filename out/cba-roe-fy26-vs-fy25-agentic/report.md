## Limitations
- CBA publishes NO quantified ROE walk or bridge chart for FY26 vs FY25 in any document searched. The earnings/equity split is my own arithmetic derivation (earnings_effect = prior ROE x earnings growth; equity_effect = residual), so its framing is not the bank's. The bank's own statement is qualitative only: 'higher cash NPAT being partly offset by higher net assets' (ev-9).
- The earnings growth rate used is the stated 7% / $730m on continuing operations (ev-10); using unrounded endpoints ($10,982m/$75,710m) would give an earnings effect of roughly +1.0ppt and a correspondingly larger equity drag. The 0.96/-0.46 split is therefore sensitive to rounding in the printed 13.5% and 7% figures.
- ROE endpoints are on a continuing operations basis; the KPI table prints identical 14.0%/13.5% cash figures including discontinued operations (ev-1), so this does not affect the movement.
- The equity_effect direction is supported by cited reasoning (retained-earnings build, no FY26 buy-back, DRP satisfied on-market) but the bank does not quantify how much of the average-equity increase came from each source.
- CBA's underlying NIM is described as 'stable' excluding liquid assets while reported NIM fell 3bpts to 2.05% (ev-11, ev-18); the earnings driver is therefore a volume-led rather than margin-led story, and the walk-level bar for this does not exist.
- The FY26 presentation's capital chart (p32) is a Dec 25 to Jun 26 CET1 walk — a different comparison and a different metric — so none of its bars were used as contributions.
