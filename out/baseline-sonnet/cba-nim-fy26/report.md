# CBA — Net Interest Margin, FY26 vs FY25

**Bank:** Commonwealth Bank of Australia
**Metric:** Group net interest margin (NIM)
**Period:** FY26 (year ended 30 June 2026)
**Comparator:** FY25 (year ended 30 June 2025)
**Basis:** Cash basis, continuing operations. This is CBA's own reported basis for NIM in all four source documents; it is not a statutory accounting measure.

## Movement

CBA's Group NIM fell **3bps**, from **2.08%** in FY25 to **2.05%** in FY26.

> "Net interest margin (%) 2.05 2.08 (3)bpts"
> — CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p.18

> "The Bank's net interest margin decreased by 3 basis points on the prior year to 2.05%."
> — CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p.28

The FY25 comparator (2.08%) is confirmed independently in CBA's own FY25 profit announcement, on the same cash, continuing-operations basis, with the same FY25 average interest earning assets figure ($1,153,684m):

> "Net interest margin (%) 2.08 1.99 9 bpts"
> — CBA-FY25-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p.18

The headline is corroborated in the ASX announcement and the results presentation:

> "Net interest margin 2.05% 3bpts on FY25"
> — CBA-FY26-asx-announcement.pdf, PDF p.2

> "NIM 2.05% (3bpts)"
> — CBA-FY26-results-presentation.pdf, PDF p.54

## Driver attribution

CBA discloses a full bridge from FY25 to FY26 NIM in the profit announcement (PDF p.28-29). The seven named drivers sum exactly to the -3bps movement, so there is **no residual**.

| Driver | Contribution | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Liquids (mix effect of growth in liquid assets) | -3bps | 85 |
| Asset pricing (lending competition) | -5bps | 95 |
| Funding costs | 0bps (flat) | 90 |
| Portfolio mix (asset and funding mix) | +2bps | 95 |
| Basis risk | 0bps (flat) | 80 |
| Capital, replicating portfolio and other (interest rate risk hedging) | +5bps | 95 |
| Treasury and Markets | -2bps | 70 |
| **Sum** | **-3bps** | |

### Liquids — -3bps — confidence 85

Average liquid assets grew $15bn (9%) year-on-year to $187bn. This growth is close to margin-neutral in dollar terms but dilutes the ratio, because liquid assets earn a lower margin than loans. CBA states that excluding this effect, NIM was stable:

> "Excluding growth in liquid assets, which have broadly neutral impacts on net interest income, net interest margin was stable."
> — CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p.28

The quantified -3bps value is the bar directly under the "Liquids" category label in the profit announcement's NIM walk chart (read by matching the chart's text-layer data label to its category label by horizontal position, since the chart does not print text in reading order). The results presentation corroborates the mechanism and a closely related figure:

> "Every additional $10bn of liquid assets is expected to reduce Group NIM by ~2bpts"
> — CBA-FY26-results-presentation.pdf, PDF p.61

### Asset pricing — -5bps — confidence 95

The largest single negative driver. Home lending pricing cut margin by 4bps and business/institutional lending pricing cut a further 1bp, both attributed to competition:

> "Asset pricing: Decreased margin by 5 basis points driven by home lending pricing (down 4 basis points) and business and institutional lending pricing (down 1 basis point), primarily reflecting the impact of competition."
> — CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p.28

This total figure is identical in the results presentation's independent chart (PDF p.60: "Asset pricing" bar = -5). That document's own footnote further splits the -4bps home lending figure into home loan competition (-3bps) and cash rate lag (-1bp), plus business & institutional lending (-1bp): -3-1-1=-5, which reconciles exactly with the profit announcement's total.

### Funding costs — 0bps (flat) — confidence 90

CBA states this driver was flat. This is a quantified nil impact, not an unmeasured one:

> "Funding costs: Flat."
> — CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p.28

### Portfolio mix — +2bps — confidence 95

Favourable asset mix from strong growth in business lending, and favourable funding mix from strong growth in deposits:

> "Portfolio mix: Increased margin by 2 basis points driven by favourable asset mix from strong growth in business lending and favourable funding mix from strong growth in deposits."
> — CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p.28

### Basis risk — 0bps (flat) — confidence 80

> "Basis risk: Flat."
> — CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p.28

The results presentation's six-bucket walk (PDF p.60) does not show a separate basis-risk line, consistent with an immaterial value, but does not independently confirm it as its own line item. Confidence is capped at 80 for this reason.

### Capital, replicating portfolio and other (interest rate risk hedging) — +5bps — confidence 95

Higher earnings on the replicating portfolio (+4bps, from portfolio rate and volume changes as swap rates rose) plus higher earnings on capital hedges (+1bp):

> "Capital, Replicating and Other: Increased margin by 5 basis points driven by higher earnings on the replicating portfolio due to changes in portfolio rate and volume (up 4 basis points) and higher earnings on capital hedges (up 1 basis point)."
> — CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p.29

The results presentation labels the same bucket "Interest rate risk hedging" and gives an identical +5bps, split identically as +4bps (replicating portfolio) / +1bp (capital):

> "In FY26, hedge earnings benefitted from higher average rates"
> — CBA-FY26-results-presentation.pdf, PDF p.61

### Treasury and Markets — -2bps — confidence 70

> "Treasury and Markets: Decreased margin by 2 basis points due to lower risk management income in Treasury and growth in reverse sale and repurchase agreement balances."
> — CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p.29

The results presentation shows this bucket at only -1bp, because it allocates the repo-balance effect (-1bp) to its own "Liquids & repos" bucket instead (see Disagreements). Confidence is lower for this driver because its exact size depends on which document's bucket boundary is used.

## Residual

**0bps.** The seven quantified drivers sum to exactly -3bps (-3 -5 +0 +2 +0 +5 -2 = -3), matching the disclosed movement with nothing left unexplained.

## Disagreement between sources

CBA's two main FY26 documents split the same combined liquids/repo/Treasury effect differently:

- **Profit announcement** (PDF p.28-29, 7 buckets): Liquids -3bps; Funding costs 0bps; Basis risk 0bps; Treasury and Markets -2bps.
- **Results presentation** (PDF p.60, 6 buckets): "Liquids & repos" -4bps (footnoted as -3bps liquid assets + -1bp institutional repos); Funding costs 0bps; no separate Basis risk line; "Treasury & Markets" -1bp.

**Preferred: the profit announcement's breakdown** (Liquids -3bps, Treasury and Markets -2bps, Basis risk 0bps).

**Reason: definitional**, not a factual conflict. Both walks total the same -3bps and agree exactly on Asset pricing (-5bps), Portfolio mix (+2bps), and the replicating-portfolio/capital-hedge bucket (+5bps). They differ only in how a combined -4bps liquids-and-repo effect is allocated across buckets. The profit announcement is preferred because it is the primary, more granular disclosure (seven named buckets rather than six) and its narrative text names both causes of the -2bps Treasury and Markets figure explicitly; the two breakdowns reconcile arithmetically once the repo effect is reallocated.

## Additional context (not used as a driver, to avoid double-counting)

CBA separately discloses a bank-defined "underlying NIM" that excludes the liquids-and-repos mix effect, which *rose* 1bp, from 2.04% (FY25, rebased) to 2.05% (FY26):

> "Rebased underlying FY25 NIM excludes 4 basis point mix impact from current period growth in average liquid assets and institutional repo balances, which have a broadly neutral impact on net interest income. FY25 headline NIM is 2.08%."
> — CBA-FY26-results-presentation.pdf, PDF p.130 (footnote to slide 6)

This is consistent with the walk above: -3bps headline, less the -4bps combined liquids-and-repos drag, leaves +1bp from the remaining named drivers.

Divisional NIM movements (all PDF p.20 of the profit announcement) point the same way directionally but are not separately reconciled to the seven group-level bps above:
- Retail Banking Services: 2.51% → 2.50% (-1bp)
- Business Banking: 3.32% → 3.39% (+7bps)
- Institutional Banking and Markets: 0.93% → 0.87% (-6bps)
- New Zealand (ASB): 2.27% → 2.30% (+3bps)

## Limitations

- The Liquids, Funding costs, and Basis risk values on the profit announcement's NIM walk chart (PDF p.28) were read as chart data-labels matched to category labels by horizontal position, because the chart's text layer is not in reading order. This mapping was cross-checked by (a) confirming all seven bucket values sum exactly to the disclosed -3bps, and (b) confirming the results presentation's independently drawn chart reconciles to the same total.
- CBA's own bucket boundaries for the liquids/repo/Treasury/basis-risk effects differ between its two FY26 documents (see Disagreements); the driver-level figures used here follow the profit announcement's more granular seven-bucket version.
- Divisional NIM movements were used only as directional corroboration, not as additional quantified group-level drivers.
- CBA-FY26-items-impacting-note.pdf is present in the same source folder but was outside the approved source list for this task and was not used.
- No independent recalculation of NIM from underlying net interest income and average interest earning assets was attempted beyond a broad consistency check (25,586 / 1,245,684 ≈ 2.05%; 24,023 / 1,153,684 ≈ 2.08%), since the exact day-count and averaging convention behind CBA's NIM calculation is not disclosed in these documents.

## Overall confidence: 85 / 100

The headline -3bps movement is stated identically across all four source documents and the FY25 comparator is independently confirmed in CBA's own FY25 disclosure. The seven quantified drivers reconcile exactly to the total movement with zero residual, and the two largest drivers (Asset pricing, Capital/Replicating/Other) are corroborated with identical values across two independent CBA documents. Confidence is capped below 95 because two of the smaller buckets (Liquids vs. Liquids & repos; Treasury and Markets) are split differently depending on which CBA document's bucket definition is used, and three of the seven values were read from a chart's positional text layer rather than running prose.
