# Q: Does Westpac's low FY25 impairment charge show that credit-risk normalisation is complete, or does the September 2025 provision stock still embed material caution? Reconcile the charge flow, the CAP/IAP movements, overlays and the probability-weighted/base/downside ECL scenarios.

*WBC, periods FY25 — confidence 20/100*

**No — the low charge is a flow story, not proof that normalisation is complete.** FY25's impairment charge fell to $424m, 5bps of average loans (FY24: $537m, 7bps; 2H25: $174m/4bps vs 1H25: $250m/6bps), but the September 2025 provision stock still embeds material caution: $1.9bn of provisions above the 100% base-case ECL, a downside scenario weight lifted to 47.5%, overlays up 33% to $238m, and net write-offs up at 6bps.

**Charge flow.** The fall was "mainly due to an increase in write-backs and recoveries partly offset by a higher CAP charge" — write-backs $195m (FY24 $93m), recoveries $247m (FY24 $190m). Cash losses did not improve: net write-offs to average gross loans rose to 6bps from 5bps.

**CAP/IAP.** The CAP charge of $458m comprised write-offs of $561m partly offset by a $103m benefit in other changes, driven by an improved commercial property/interest-rate outlook and mortgage 90+ delinquencies falling from 1.05% to 0.70% — held back by a 5ppt increase in the downside weight and new portfolio overlays. Modelled CAPs still fell $168m over the year. IAPs netted a $34m benefit (new IAPs $408m; write-backs $195m; recoveries $247m); 2H25 new IAPs fell to $157m (1H25 $251m) and IAP stock fell $72m on a single trade-sector write-off.

**Overlays.** Overlay stock reached $238m (Mar-25 $130m; Sep-24 $179m), +$59m over the year and +$108m in 2H25 — new overlays for "portfolio seasoning and geographical areas experiencing higher stress not included in modelled outcomes", partly offset by releases (construction, Australian mortgages, consumer finance).

**Scenarios.** Reported probability-weighted ECL of $4,987m sits between 100% base-case ECL of $3,040m and 100% downside ECL of $7,152m — "$1.9bn in provisions above the base case ECL". The downside weight rose from 42.5% (Sep-24) through 45.0% (Mar-25) to 47.5%; base 50.0%. Downside trough assumptions: GDP (6%), unemployment 11%, residential property (27%), commercial property (32%). Sep-25 stack: Stage 2 CAP $2,087m, Stage 3 CAP $933m, Stage 3 IAP $539m, overlay $238m.

**Bottom line.** Credit quality is genuinely improving — stressed exposures down 17bps to 1.28% of TCE — and management says it remains "appropriately provisioned" ($4,987m; CAP/credit RWA 1.25%). The caution is by design (scenario weights and overlays), not slack awaiting release; the documents do not state normalisation is complete, nor guide on whether charges stay at 4–5bps.
