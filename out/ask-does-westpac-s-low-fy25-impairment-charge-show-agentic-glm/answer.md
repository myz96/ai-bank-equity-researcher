# Q: Does Westpac's low FY25 impairment charge show that credit-risk normalisation is complete, or does the September 2025 provision stock still embed material caution? Reconcile the charge flow, the CAP/IAP movements, overlays and the probability-weighted/base/downside ECL scenarios.

*WBC, periods FY25 — confidence 88/100*

**Direct answer:** No — the low FY25 charge does not, on its own, show credit-risk normalisation is complete. The September 2025 provision stock still embeds material caution: the downside scenario weight was raised to 47.5%, overlays grew to $238m, and total ECL of $4,987m stands "$1.9bn in provisions above the base case ECL" of $3,040m.

**Charge flow.** The FY25 impairment charge was $424m, 5bps of average loans (FY24: $537m, 7bps). It comprised a CAP charge of $458m — write-offs of $561m partly offset by a $103m benefit in other changes in CAP — and a net IAP benefit of $34m (new IAPs $408m, write-backs $195m, recoveries $247m). The improvement came from write-backs and recoveries (the IAP line swung from $(140)m in FY24 to +$34m), not from lower losses: net write-offs rose to 6bps from 5bps, with write-offs largely in credit cards and personal loans. The 2H25 charge was $174m (4bps vs 6bps in 1H25).

**CAP/IAP stock.** Total ECL fell 2% to $4,987m (Sep-24: $5,096m). Modelled CAP fell $168m on an improved commercial-property/interest-rate outlook, lower mortgage 90+ delinquencies (1.05%→0.70%) and the auto finance sale — partly offset by a 5ppt increase in the downside weight (geopolitical instability, trade policies). Overlays rose $59m to $238m: new overlays for portfolio seasoning and geographies "not included in modelled outcomes", partly offset by releases (construction, Australian mortgages, consumer finance). IAPs were flat at $539m (2H: −$72m on a single trade-sector write-off).

**Scenarios.** Reported probability-weighted ECL of $4,987m sits between 100% base-case ECL of $3,040m and 100% downside ECL of $7,152m. Weights: base 50.0%, downside 47.5% (Sep-24: 42.5%), upside 2.5%. Downside severity (trough/peak): GDP −6%, unemployment 11%, residential property −27%, commercial property −32% (forecast date 18 September 2025).

**Reading.** Credit metrics improved (stressed exposures 1.28% vs 1.45%; provisions to gross loans 58bps vs 63bps), but the bank kept adding caution: 2H overlays +$108m and a further +2.5ppt downside weight, and B&W's 2H charge (21bps) explicitly reflects "higher overlays and an increase in the downside scenario weight". A low charge with a rising downside weight and growing overlays says the stock, not the flow, carries the caution.

**Not settled:** whether management regards normalisation as complete (no such statement in the pages read); the $1.9bn is a scenario construct, not a loss forecast; overlay composition by portfolio is not disclosed on the pages read.
