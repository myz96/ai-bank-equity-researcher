# WBC FY25: low charge, but the provision stock still embeds material caution

**Answer: the September 2025 provision stock still embeds material caution. The low charge does not show a completed normalisation of credit risk. It shows benign loss emergence net of one-off recoveries, while management added caution to the stock.**

## 1. The charge flow reconciles to gross losses minus large offsets

FY25 charge: $424m, 5bps of average loans (FY24: $537m, 7bps) (RA p21). It reconciles as:

| $m, FY25 | Charge / (benefit) |
|---|---|
| Write-offs (CAP) | (561) |
| New IAPs | (408) |
| Write-backs | 195 |
| Recoveries | 247 |
| Other changes in CAP | 103 |
| **Total** | **(424)** |

Gross loss flow ($969m) did not fall much (FY24 new IAPs $423m, write-offs $486m). The lower charge came from write-backs and recoveries (+$442m, up from $283m). Net write-offs to average loans rose to 6bps from 5bps, and new impaired inflows rose 19% to $831m (RA p21-22). So the flow improvement is partly non-recurring offsets, not lower loss emergence.

## 2. The stock: modelled release, offset by deliberate caution

Total ECL fell only 2% to $4,987m (RA p24):

- Modelled CAP fell $168m to $4,201m on better commercial-property, rate and delinquency outlooks.
- Overlays rose $59m YoY (and $108m HoH) to $238m: new overlays for portfolio seasoning and stressed geographies, net of construction / mortgage / consumer-finance releases.
- The downside scenario weight rose 5ppt over the year to 47.5% (upside cut to 2.5%); this went through "other changes in CAP", so the $103m benefit is after this add.
- IAPs flat YoY at $539m; the $72m HoH fall is a single-name trade-sector write-off, not a release.

## 3. Scenario ECL: $1.9bn above base case

Reported probability-weighted ECL of $4,987m sits $1.9bn above 100% base-case ECL ($3,040m) and $2.2bn below 100% downside ($7,152m) (Pres p54). The downside is severe: unemployment peak 11%, commercial property -32%. Roughly 39% of the stock is scenario-weighting and overlay caution, not base-case expected loss.

## 4. The counterweight: coverage ratios drifted down

Provisions to gross loans fell to 58bps (63bps), CAP to credit RWA to 1.25% (1.30%), impaired coverage to 39.5% (41.3%) (RA p22). Caution was preserved in weights and overlays, but the stock did not keep pace with 6% loan growth. Part of the metric improvement is also definitional: mortgage delinquency falls reflect "a change to serviceability treatment", and consumer delinquency falls reflect the auto-portfolio sale (RA p23).

**Judgment.** Credit quality genuinely improved (stressed exposures 1.28% of TCE from 1.45%), but management's own actions — a 47.5% downside weight, higher overlays, a $1.9bn buffer above base case — say normalisation is not declared complete. The low charge and the cautious stock coexist; a release of that caution remains a future P&L tailwind, not a delivered one.

**Confidence: 85/100.**
