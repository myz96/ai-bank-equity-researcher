# WBC FY26: productivity target versus cost pressure

**Judgment: the >$500m FY26 productivity target is sized as an offset to wage, banker and UNITE pressure, not as a jaws-restorer. The disclosure gives no FY26 revenue guidance, so positive jaws cannot be demonstrated from these documents.**

## FY25 expense bridge (presentation p27, $m)

| FY24 | Staff | Technology | Volume/other | Productivity | Investments | Restructuring | FY25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10,944 | +397 | +146 | +199 | (402) | +359 | +273 | 11,916 |

Expenses rose 9% (6% ex the $273m Fit for Growth restructuring charge) against income growth of 3% ($22,464m vs $21,763m) — jaws of roughly −6ppts (−3ppts ex charge, derived). Gross cost growth ex restructuring was $1,101m; the $402m of productivity delivered offset only ~37% of it. CTI ex Notable Items rose from 50.3% to 53.0%.

## Investment mix and UNITE profile

| $m | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| UNITE | 147 | 660 | $850–950m, ~75% expensed |
| Growth & productivity | 550 | 563 | reduction expected |
| Risk & regulatory | 1,059 | 695 | reduction expected |
| **Total** | **1,756** | **1,918** | **~$2bn** |
| % expensed | 56% | 60% | — |
| Amortisation | 889 | 995 | increase "to moderate" |

UNITE is multi-year: the plan "extends into FY29", FY27–FY28 absorb c.40% of the c.$2bn p.a. total spend, with lower spend only in FY29 and c.75% expensed throughout. FY25 UNITE expensing was 74% (results announcement p19) or 76% (presentation p15); I prefer the tabulated 74%.

## Weighing FY26

Derived from disclosed figures: expensed UNITE rises from ~$488m (74% × $660m) to ~$638–713m (75% × $850–950m), a ~$150–225m headwind. On top sit the flagged EBA increase of 3–4% and banker investment (FY25 staff costs alone rose $397m, or 7%, on wage growth, UNITE and bankers) and further, if moderating, amortisation growth. The >$500m target is only ~$100m above the $402m delivered in FY25 — a year in which the same construct still produced 6% ex-charge expense growth. The target therefore roughly matches the identified headwinds; any absolute cost-base decline would depend on the non-repeat of the $273m charge, which is not guided. Westpac itself frames closing the CTI gap to peers (3.7ppts above, 1H25) as a Sep-29 outcome via UNITE, not an FY26 one.

**Conclusion:** productivity is mainly the funding mechanism that keeps ex-charge expense growth from re-running FY25's 6% while UNITE spend peaks; restoring positive jaws in FY26 would require income growth accelerating above FY25's 3%, on which these documents are silent.

**Confidence: 80/100**

## Limitations

- No FY26 revenue or NIM guidance is disclosed, so jaws cannot be computed prospectively.
- No FY26 total expense-growth guidance; only component flags.
- The EBA base within the $6,326m staff line is not split out.
- Recurrence or not of the $273m restructuring charge is not addressed.
- UNITE expensed share differs between documents (74% vs 76%).
