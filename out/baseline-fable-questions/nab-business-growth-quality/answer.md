# NAB FY25: Was B&PB growth value-accretive?

**Verdict: Yes, on balance.** Growth was broad-based, deposit-funded, and held returns per unit of risk flat — but it was not free: 8bps of NIM went to competition and deposit mix, jaws were slightly negative, and the NPL stock rose materially. The credit deterioration sits in the stock, not the FY25 charge, and the 2H25 impaired spike is substantially two Agri single names.

| B&PB (restated) | FY25 | FY24 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| GLAs | $279.5bn | $261.7bn | +6.8% |
| — Business lending | $166.3bn | $155.0bn | +7.3% |
| Net operating income | $8,531m | $8,358m | +2.1% |
| Cash earnings | $3,330m | $3,277m | +1.6% |
| NIM | 3.01% | 3.09% | −8bps |
| CIC / GLAs | 0.19% | 0.20% | −1bp |
| Cash earnings / avg RWA | 2.18% | 2.19% | −1bp |
| Cost to income | 37.9% | 37.6% | +30bps |
| Impaired / GLAs | 0.49% | 0.39% | +10bps |
| Default-not-impaired / GLAs | 2.05% | 1.74% | +31bps |

(Results book, PDF p.36)

**Reconciliation.** Volume did the work: average interest-earning assets +5.4% converted to NII of only +2.8% because NIM gave up 8bps to "competitive pressures impacting both the business and housing lending portfolios, as well as higher deposit costs" (results book p.37). The margin stabilised in 2H25 (3.01%→3.02%). Other income fell 2.9% (of which $22m was the NZ wealth disposal). Costs +2.9% (bankers, technology) left underlying profit +1.6%; the CIC rose only $6m, so cash earnings also grew 1.6%. Deposits +6.6% to $237.9bn broadly funded the $17.8bn of lending growth.

**Margin vs credit as the price of growth.** The P&L credit cost did not rise with volume: CIC/GLAs fell 1bp to 0.19%, and the business-lending charge fell YoY ($209m+$308m halves in FY24 vs $165m+$266m in FY25 — presentation p.95); the FY25 increase was housing. The stock deteriorated: B&PB NPEs reached $7,079m, 2.54% of GLAs (2.12% Sep 24), and business-lending NPLs 3.22% of business GLAs (2.66%). RWA grew 9.0% — faster than GLAs — partly from "deterioration in asset quality", yet cash earnings/avg RWA held at 2.18% and combined Business Banking ROE is disclosed at 18%.

**Broad-based vs single-name.** Growth: broad-based — "business lending across a broad range of sectors" (results book p.24); Agri +3.5%, Health +5.9%, CRE +7.4%, Other +10.4%, small business +9.2%; SME share up to 28.0% (presentation p.50). Deterioration: the FY-long default-not-impaired build (+31bps) is portfolio-wide, but the Sep-25 impaired spike is single-name-skewed — two large, well-secured Agri customers add 21bps to the 3.22% NPL ratio, excluding which the ratio fell 3bps HoH to 3.01%; watch loans fell 9bps, the first decline since Mar 23 (presentation p.26-27). Underwriting looks held: 73% fully secured, 6% unsecured, sub-investment-grade share 77% vs 78% (presentation p.95).

**Net:** value-accretive growth at broadly flat risk-adjusted returns, with modest margin/cost dilution — not growth bought with credit standards.

Confidence: 80/100.
