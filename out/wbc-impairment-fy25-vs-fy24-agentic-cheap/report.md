# WBC — impairment — FY25 vs FY24

**Movement (ex_notables basis):** 537$m → 424$m (-113$m) | **Attribution confidence:** 40/100

Westpac's credit impairment charge fell $113 million to $424 million in FY25 from $537 million in FY24, a 21% reduction. The loss rate declined 2 basis points to 5 bps of average loans (from 7 bps). The improvement was driven primarily by a $174 million favourable shift in individually assessed provisions — write-backs rose $102 million to $195 million and recoveries rose $57 million to $247 million, partly offset by new IAPs falling only $15 million to $408 million. This was partially offset by a $61 million adverse move in collectively assessed provisions, where write-offs rose $75 million to $561 million, partly offset by other CAP changes improving $14 million to a $103 million benefit. Divisionally, Consumer improved $31 million ($217m vs $248m), Institutional improved $121 million ($1m benefit vs $120m charge), and New Zealand improved $66 million ($41m benefit vs $25m charge), while Business & Wealth worsened $103 million ($245m vs $142m) due to higher overlays, downside scenario increases, and new IAPs in wholesale & retail trade.
