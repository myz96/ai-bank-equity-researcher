# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column Full Year Ended 30 Jun 25 -> column Full Year Ended 30 Jun 26*

CBA's Group net interest margin fell 3 bps in FY26 to 2.05% from 2.08% in FY25 (results book KPI table p28 and appendix p100; corroborated in the FY25 book p100 and the results presentation p60). The bank states the decrease was driven by asset pricing (-5 bps, competition) and growth in liquid assets, partly offset by capital/replicating (+5 bps) and portfolio mix (+2 bps). Excluding growth in liquid assets, which have broadly neutral impacts on net interest income, the bank says NIM was stable. The results book's NIM walk (p28) is the primary framing: Liquids -3, Asset pricing -5, Funding costs 0, Portfolio mix +2, Basis risk 0, Capital Replicating +5, Treasury & Markets -2, summing to -3 bps.
