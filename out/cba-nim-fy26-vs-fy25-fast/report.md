CBA's Group net interest margin fell 3 basis points to 2.05% in FY26 from 2.08% in FY25 (cash basis, continuing operations). The results book's walk attributes the decline to asset pricing (-5 bps, home lending down 4 bps and business/institutional lending down 1 bps on competition) and growth in liquid assets (-3 bps), partly offset by higher earnings on the replicating portfolio and capital hedges (+5 bps) and favourable portfolio mix (+2 bps). Excluding growth in liquid assets, which have broadly neutral impacts on net interest income, the margin was stable. The results presentation corroborates the same -3 bps movement (2.08% to 2.05%) and the same key drivers.
