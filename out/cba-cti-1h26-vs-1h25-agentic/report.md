The three ppt contributions are MY arithmetic on the disclosed levels of both periods, not the bank's own decomposition: income effect = 6,372/15,021 - 6,372/14,097 = -2.78ppt; underlying expense effect = (6,720-6,372)/15,021 = +2.32ppt; notable items = 170/15,021 = +1.13ppt. - The primary walk I read (profit announcement p31, ev-9) is an OPERATING EXPENSE walk in $m, not a ratio walk. Its bars (Inflation +275, Investment in technology +222, Investment in frontline and operations +78, Other -6, Productivity -221) decompose the $348m underlying expense increase only; they explain the composition of the expense_growth driver and are NOT ppt contributions to the ratio. The walk also excludes the $170m of notable items entirely.
- The presentation's 'Contribution to mvt' figures on p28 (Inflation +4.3%, Investment in technology +3.5%, Investment in frontline and operations +1.2%, Productivity (3.4%), ev-23) are percentage contributions to UNDERLYING EXPENSE GROWTH, not to the cost-to-income ratio, and are quoted in the expense driver narrative as such.
- The Productivity bar of -$221m and the '(3.4%)' contribution are net offsets that the bank flags inside its other bars: CBA says staff, IT and other expense increases were each 'partly offset by productivity initiatives' (ev-13, ev-14, ev-33). The gross investment and inflation spend is therefore larger than the net +$348m increase implies.
- CBA states that excluding liquid assets and institutional reverse repos the underlying NIM was UNCHANGED (ev-30), so the reported 4bpt NIM decline overstates the drag on income growth; the income contribution I compute is a net number and I cannot split it between volume and margin from the evidence read.
- I did not read the 2H25-comparison expense walk on results_presentation p65; it describes the half-on-half movement and is out of scope for this task.
- No evidence read states whether total operating income itself contained notable items in either period; the 44.7% underlying ratio implies underlying income is approximately equal to total income ($6,720/0.447 = $15,033m vs $15,021m printed), consistent with no material income-side notables, but the bank does not print an 'underlying operating income' level on the pages read.
