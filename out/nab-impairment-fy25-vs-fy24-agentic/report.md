## Limitations

- The only provision walk in the corpus (investor presentation p85, ev-35) is a provision BALANCE walk and runs Mar 25 -> Sep 25 (context), not FY24 -> FY25. Its bars (underlying CP -26, Forward Looking Economic Adjustment +96, Target sector FLAs -185, IAP +243) are NOT drivers of this task's movement and are excluded.
- Forward-looking adjustments/overlays are named by NAB as a cause of the collective write-back but are NOT separately quantified for FY25 vs FY24, so no 'overlays_fla' driver could be given a contribution; the $89m net FLA release figure the presentation prints (ev-37) belongs to the Mar 25 -> Sep 25 half, not the full-year comparison.
- The collective line is a NET number and NAB explicitly qualifies it: the release from forward-looking provisions and from transfers to individually assessed was 'partially offset by' B&PB business-lending volume growth and asset-quality deterioration across the Australian lending portfolio (ev-14). The volume and risk-migration sub-parts inside that bar are not separately sized for FY25 v FY24, so collective.volume could not be split out from collective.asset_quality.
- Divisional CIC deltas were computed by me from the two period columns of each divisional table (C&IB +153, Corporate Functions -83 write-back, B&PB +6, Personal Banking -33, NZ -104; sum +105). The results book states only the Corporate Functions delta in words (-$83m, ev-25); the others are my arithmetic. Divisions are the where-layer, not provision types, so they inform narratives rather than the bridge.
- NZ Banking's FY25/FY24 charge is quoted in AUD ($25m vs $129m, ev-22) to be additive with the Group; in NZD the division prints NZ$27m vs NZ$141m, and NAB states FX had nil impact on the NZ credit impairment charge.
- Corroboration in the second document is partial: the investor presentation prints Group CIC only half-yearly (p26, p81, p82) and confirms divisional FY totals only for C&IB (ev-33) and Personal Banking (ev-43) via half-year bars; it publishes no FY-on-FY Group provision-type table.
- Capped at 80: individual_provisions +329 $m, write_backs_recoveries -1 $m. The records these claims cite do not state those numbers, so each one is arithmetic over the evidence rather than a figure read from it.
