NAB publishes only a half-on-half walk (Mar 25→Sep 25: cash earnings +82bps, dividend -61bps, credit RWA -45bps, other RWA +1bp, other -8bps; ev-8, ev-15), so I do not restate those bars as FY contributions. Pro-forma 11.81% post the MLC Life stake sale is a different measure (ev-30, ev-47).

## Limitations
- NAB publishes NO FY24→FY25 CET1 walk. Both the results book (ev-8/ev-15) and the presentation (ev-8 equivalent slide) show only the Mar 25→Sep 25 half-on-half walk, which read_chart confirmed is 'context', not the task comparison. Its bars are therefore described in narratives, never used as FY contributions.
- The -75bps total-RWA contribution is MY arithmetic (Sep 25 CET1 capital of $51,527m divided by Sep 24 RWA of $413,946m versus Sep 25 RWA of $440,557m); NAB does not publish a FY-on-FY bps split, so the framing is not the bank's and confidence is capped accordingly.
- Only the buy-back (-15bps) carries a bank-stated FY25 bps impact (ev-28). Model/methodology, capital floor, earnings, dividends and non-credit RWA are unquantified in bps for the FY window; the bps figures cited for them (-13bps models, -3bps floor, +82/-61bps earnings/dividend) belong to the Mar 25–Sep 25 half only.
- Residual: quantified FY drivers (-75bps RWA, -15bps buy-back = -90bps) exceed the -65bps movement because the offsetting positive from retained earnings and other capital build (+$388m of CET1 capital, ev-17) is not separately quantified by NAB in bps; the ~+25bps residual is that unquantified capital-generation offset. Signs are not forced to fit.
- Credit RWA (rwa.credit) is a component of the total-RWA driver, not an addition to it; the two must not be summed.
- The $4.8bn off-balance-sheet overlay is disclosed by NAB as sitting inside the half's 'Models & methodology' credit RWA driver (ev-45), so the credit RWA bar is a net number blending volume growth, asset quality and this overlay; NAB does not split the overlay's effect on a FY basis.
- APS 117 IRRBB revisions took effect 1 October 2025 — AFTER the 30 Sep 25 balance date — so they do not affect this movement (ev-14, ev-40).
- The presentation does not state the FY25 v FY24 CET1 movement in words; corroboration of the -65bps came from two places within the results book (ev-1 capital table, ev-6/ev-28/ev-29 KPI page), with the presentation corroborating levels and component RWA only (ev-50, ev-104-series).
- No published walk covers FY25 vs FY24: the bank's walk for this metric describes another comparison, so the driver split is not walk-verified for this comparison. Confidence is capped at 85.
