# CBA — cet1 — FY21 vs FY20

**Movement (cash basis):** 1160bps → 1310bps (+150bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 88/100

*Read from: row 'Common Equity Tier 1 (APRA) (%)', column 30 Jun 20 (Full Year Ended) -> column 30 Jun 21 (Full Year Ended)*

CBA's APRA Level 2 CET1 ratio rose 150 bpts, from 11.6% at 30 Jun 20 to 13.1% at 30 Jun 21, well above APRA's 'unquestionably strong' 10.5% benchmark (ev-55, ev-2, ev-62). CET1 capital grew $52,551m to $58,836m while total RWA fell 1% to $450,680m from $454,948m (ev-41, ev-57). CBA's own FY20→FY21 walk attributes the move to cash NPAT (+88 bpts 2H20, +97 bpts 2H21), divestments (+42), the APRA operational risk add-on reduction (+17), RWA (-13 then +8) and other (-2 then +4), less dividends (-32 and -59) (ev-49). The internationally comparable CET1 ratio — a different measure — rose 200 bpts to 19.4% (ev-56). The FY21 dividend was 350c, up 17%, totalling $6,209m against $5,275m (ev-58, ev-60). A $6.0bn off-market buy-back was announced but sits after the balance date, reducing the ratio only pro-forma to 12.1–12.2% (ev-65, ev-26).

## Limitations
- The results book (profit announcement) publishes only a HALF-ON-HALF CET1 walk (Dec 20 to Jun 21: +97 cash NPAT, +8 RWA, +4 other, -59 dividend, ev-7). No FY20-to-FY21 walk appears in the results book, so the driver table is built from the results PRESENTATION appendix (p111), a slide - one rung lower in the source hierarchy.
- The adopted p111 walk decomposes the year via the Dec 20 intermediate point, so each driver is the sum of two half-year bars rather than a single bank-stated full-year contribution. The endpoints and the +150 bpt total are the task's own, and the bars sum to +150.
- CBA calls its p37 aggregate bar 'Organic (net of growth / investment, ex dividends)' (+180 bpts): a NET number in which franchise growth and investment spend are already offset against earnings, so the gross earnings contribution is larger than any single bar shows (ev-28, ev-27).
- The Cash NPAT bars explicitly EXCLUDE net equity accounted profits from associates, 'as they are capital neutral with offsetting increases in regulatory capital deductions' (ev-3), so that earnings stream appears in neither the earnings nor the deductions driver.
- No document gives a bpts split of the RWA bars between credit, market, operational and IRRBB risk on an FY21-vs-FY20 basis; the RWA driver narrative uses the $M levels from the capital note (ev-33, ev-59) and the bpts split published on p35 of the presentation is a half-on-half figure and is not used.
- The 2H20 half of the walk (Jun 20 to Dec 20) is not itself the task comparison; it is used only as a component of the full-year span, consistent with the walk CBA publishes.
- Capped at 80: earnings_generation +185 bps, dividend_net_drp -91 bps, rwa -5 bps, capital_returns +0 bps. The records these claims cite do not state those numbers, so each one is arithmetic over the evidence rather than a figure read from it.
