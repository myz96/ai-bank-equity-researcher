# NAB — impairment — FY25 vs FY24

**Movement (cash basis):** 728$m → 833$m (+105$m) | **Attribution confidence:** 85/100

*Read from: row 'Credit impairment charge', column Year to Sep 24 $m -> column Year to Sep 25 $m*

NAB's credit impairment charge (CIC) increased by $105 million, or 14.4%, to $833 million in FY25 from $728 million in FY24. The increase was driven by a $328 million rise in individually assessed charges (from $636m to $964m), primarily reflecting impairments for a small number of larger customers in Corporate & Institutional Banking and growth in Business & Private Banking business lending, partially offset by a $223 million improvement in collective provisions (from a $92m charge to a $131m write-back), driven by a $283 million release of forward-looking provisions including target sector FLAs.
