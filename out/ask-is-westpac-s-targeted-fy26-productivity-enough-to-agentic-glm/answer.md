# Q: Is Westpac's targeted FY26 productivity enough to restore positive jaws, or is it mainly an offset to continuing wage, banker and UNITE investment pressure? Ground the judgment in the FY25 expense bridge, the investment-spend mix and the disclosed duration/expensing profile of UNITE.

*WBC, periods FY25 — confidence 78/100*

**Mainly an offset — the FY25 documents do not show it restoring positive jaws.** All figures are FY25 (year ended 30 Sep 2025) or FY24 comparatives; FY26 items are the pack's forward-looking statements.

In the FY25 expense bridge (FY24 $10,944m → FY25 $11,916m), productivity of −$402m was fully absorbed by staff costs +$397m (EBA increase 3% to 4%, superannuation, investment in bankers), investments +$359m (UNITE $399m, amortisation +$106m), technology +$146m and volume/other +$199m, plus a $273m Fit for Growth restructuring charge. Expenses ex-restructuring rose 6% against 3% revenue growth (ex Notable Items); the cost-to-income ratio ex Notable Items worsened 3ppts to 53.0%. No FY26 income or expense guidance is disclosed from which positive jaws could be shown.

The 2H25 bridge repeats the shape: productivity −$249m against investments +$208m, staff +$143m and restructuring +$273m. Staff expenses rose 7% to $6,326m with average FTE +1% — banker investment outweighing productivity headcount reductions.

Investment mix: FY25 spend $1,918m (+9%) — UNITE $660m (74% expensed; 34% of spend), growth & productivity $563m, risk & regulatory $695m (−34%); expensed share up 4ppts to 60%; amortisation $995m (FY24 $889m). FY26 expectations: UNITE $850–950m, ~75% expensed, c.40% of ~$2bn total spend; reductions in growth & productivity and risk & regulation; amortisation increase "to moderate".

UNITE duration: the plan extends into FY29, with lower spend in FY29 and c.75% of spend expensed; UNITE is ~40% of total investment over FY25 to 1H29 ($807m spent to Sep-25). Disclosed benefits are small so far — two completed initiatives carry ~$5m and ~$4m annual savings; current focus areas cost c.$265m for ~$70m expected benefits. With ~75% expensed, UNITE's P&L drag peaks in FY26 before benefits mature.

Derived arithmetic (mine, not bank-stated): UNITE expensed rises ~$140–220m; 3–4% EBA on the $6,326m staff base implies ~$190–250m before banker investment; amortisation still rises. Against >$500m targeted productivity, expense growth ex-restructuring could be roughly flat — but the non-repeating $273m charge flatters reported growth by ~2.3ppts, and whether >$500m is incremental to FY25's $402m is undisclosed.

Not settled: "jaws" is never named; no FY26 revenue guidance exists; the peer cost-to-income goal (3.7ppts above peers at Sep-25) is a Sep-29 target, not FY26.
