# CBA — nim — FY21 vs FY20

**Movement (cash basis):** 207bps → 203bps (-4bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 93/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column 30 Jun 20 (Full Year Ended) -> column 30 Jun 21 (Full Year Ended)*

CBA's Group net interest margin fell 4 basis points in FY21 to 2.03%, from 2.07% in FY20, on a cash, continuing-operations basis (ev-18, ev-1, ev-16). Net interest income still rose $229 million or 1% to $18,839 million, because a $32 billion or 4% increase in average interest earning assets to $930 billion more than offset the margin decline (ev-2). The whole of the decline is explained by balance-sheet liquidity: higher liquids cost 4 bpts, which CBA says had "Minimal impact on NII" (ev-14), leaving the margin "Flat ex liquids – pressure from lower interest rates, offset by favourable portfolio mix, lower wholesale funding costs" (ev-13). Within the rest, asset pricing (-2), deposit pricing and funding (-3) and capital and other (-2) were offset by portfolio mix (+2), basis risk incl. replicating portfolio (+3) and Treasury & Markets (+2) (ev-12). On an including-discontinued-operations basis the same row prints 2.03% vs 2.08%, a 5 bpt fall (ev-19).

### asset_pricing — "Asset Pricing"
*-2 bps | confidence 92/100*

## Source disagreements
- **FY21 vs FY20 Group NIM movement: continuing operations vs including discontinued operations** (definitional): (4)bpts: 2.03% vs 2.07% (continuing operations) vs (5)bpts: 2.03% vs 2.08% (including discontinued operations)
  Preferred: (4)bpts: 2.03% vs 2.07% (continuing operations). The FY21 KPI table prints the NIM row twice. The continuing-operations block is CBA's headline framing, matches the Group NII/NIM analysis table and the FY20 book's own 2.07% comparative, and is the basis of the published seven-bar walk. The including-discontinued row differs only in the FY20 base (2.08%).

## Limitations
- The results book publishes its FY20->FY21 decomposition as narrative paragraphs rather than as a chart; the seven quantified bars were corroborated by the results presentation p63 waterfall (FY20 207 -> FY21 203), which passed the sum check and matched the book bar-for-bar, so no framing conflict arose.
- CBA qualifies the -4 bpt liquids bar as having 'Minimal impact on NII': the margin dilution is largely mechanical denominator growth from a $17bn rise in average liquid assets, not an earnings loss, and CBA describes the margin as 'Flat ex liquids'. Read the headline -4 bpts with that qualification.
- The +3 bpt basis risk bar is labelled 'Basis Risk (incl RP)' on the slide, so it may bundle replicating-portfolio effects; the book separately reports a +2 bpt replicating portfolio benefit inside the funding costs bar, and CBA does not reconcile these labels.
- Sub-part figures inside each driver (e.g. home loan repricing +9 bpts, deposit cash-rate impact -7 bpts) are stated only in the FY21 documents; they are not independently corroborated in the FY20 book.
