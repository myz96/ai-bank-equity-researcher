# CBA — nim — FY21 vs FY20

**Movement (cash basis):** 207bps → 203bps (-4bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 93/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column 30 Jun 20 full year column -> column 30 Jun 21 full year column*

CBA's Group net interest margin (continuing operations, cash basis) fell 4bpts to 2.03% in FY21 from 2.07% in FY20 (ev-24, ev-1). Including discontinued operations the same KPI table prints 2.03% vs 2.08%, a 5bpt decline (ev-25); the FY20 book had reported FY20 continuing-operations NIM at 2.07%, so the comparator is unrestated (ev-30). Net interest income still rose $229m or 1% to $18,839m, as a $32bn/4% rise in average interest earning assets to $930bn outweighed the margin fall (ev-2). The bank frames the decline as almost entirely liquids-driven: margin was "Flat ex liquids – pressure from lower interest rates, offset by favourable portfolio mix, lower wholesale funding costs", with the higher-liquids bar carrying "Minimal impact on NII" (ev-27, ev-12). The seven-bar walk (liquids -4, asset pricing -2, deposit pricing & funding -3, capital & other -2, portfolio mix +2, basis risk +3, Treasury & Markets +2) sums exactly to -4bpts (ev-11).

## Source disagreements
- **FY20 comparator NIM level / size of decline** (definitional): 2.07% continuing operations, (4)bpts vs 2.08% including discontinued operations, (5)bpts
  Preferred: 2.07% continuing operations, (4)bpts. The FY21 KPI table prints both. The walk and the Group performance analysis are "Presented on a continuing operations basis", so the continuing-operations 2.07%->2.03% (-4bpts) is the framing that the bank's own decomposition explains (ev-24, ev-25, ev-11).

## Limitations
- The bank qualifies the -4bpt liquids bar as having "Minimal impact on NII" (ev-12) and describes the margin as "Flat ex liquids" (ev-27): the headline decline is largely balance-sheet dilution from holding more low-yielding liquid assets, not lost earnings, so the -4bpts overstates the economic deterioration.
- Sub-part figures inside the bars come from narrative/annotations and do not always tie exactly across documents: the results book prints deposit cash-rate impact as -7bpts while the slide annotation prints -8bpts (with Investment +1bpt), a presentational rounding/allocation difference within the same -3bpt net bar.
- Both the adopted framing (results book narrative, pp.12-13) and the corroborating slide walk (p.63) use identical bar values, so no independent third quantification of the walk was available.
- FY21 and FY20 average interest earning assets are net of mortgage offset balances and exclude assets held for sale, so the margin denominator basis differs from the gross balances printed in the accounts (ev-1 footnotes).
