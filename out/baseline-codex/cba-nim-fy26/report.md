# Commonwealth Bank of Australia: FY26 net interest margin attribution

## Conclusion

CBA's Group NIM fell 3 basis points to 2.05% in FY26 from 2.08% in FY25. The cash-basis KPI table reports "2.05", "2.08" and "(3)bpts" (CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 18, "Net interest margin (%) 2.05 2.08 (3)bpts"). The FY25 profit announcement independently confirms the starting point (CBA-FY25-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 28, "Net interest margin (%) 2.08"). The results presentation and ASX announcement agree (CBA-FY26-results-presentation.pdf, PDF p. 55, "2.08% 2.05% ... (3bpts)"; CBA-FY26-asx-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 2, "2.05% ... 3bpts on FY25").

The reported decline was largely denominator dilution from liquid assets and institutional repos, plus lending-price pressure. These negatives were substantially offset by higher replicating-portfolio and capital-hedge earnings and favourable portfolio mix. Excluding liquid-asset growth, CBA says NIM was stable (CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 28, "Excluding growth in liquid assets ... net interest margin was stable"). The broader summary also says margins were broadly stable excluding liquid assets and institutional reverse repos (CBA-FY26-asx-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 2, "Margins were broadly stable excluding growth in liquid assets and institutional reverse sale and repurchase agreements").

## Basis and comparator

- Basis: Group NIM in the cash-basis KPI presentation, on a continuing-operations basis. The KPI note says "Presented on a cash basis unless stated otherwise" (CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 18), while the detailed section is headed "Net Interest Income (continuing operations basis)" (CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 28). CBA does not show a separate statutory NIM in these disclosures.

- Comparator: full year ended 30 June 2026 versus full year ended 30 June 2025 (CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 28, "Full Year Ended 30 Jun 26 30 Jun 25").

- Definition: Group NIM is "Net interest income divided by average interest earning assets for the full year or the half year annualised" (CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 100).

## Bridge and residual

The quantified components sum to the reported movement:

`-3 - 1 - 3 - 1 - 1 + 0 + 2 + 0 + 4 + 1 - 1 = -3bp`

Reported movement: -3bp. Quantified-driver sum: -3bp. Residual: **0bp**. The residual is an arithmetic calculation from CBA's whole-basis-point bridge, not a separately disclosed bank figure.

## Source presentation difference

There is one classification difference, not an economic inconsistency:

- The profit announcement shows liquid assets at -3bp in its bridge (CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 28, "Liquids (0.03%)") and reports Treasury & Markets at -2bp (CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 29, "Treasury and Markets: Decreased margin by 2 basis points"), including reverse-repo growth.

- The presentation separates the repo effect: liquid assets -3bp, institutional repos -1bp, and remaining Treasury & Markets -1bp (CBA-FY26-results-presentation.pdf, PDF p. 60, "Increase in liquid assets (3)", "Increase in institutional repos (1)", and "Treasury & Markets (1)").

I prefer the presentation taxonomy for the detailed attribution because it is more granular and its complete bridge sums to the headline -3bp. Both sources reconcile economically: -3bp plus -2bp equals -3bp plus -1bp plus -1bp.

The home-lending presentation is also more granular rather than conflicting: the profit announcement gives -4bp for home-lending pricing (CBA-FY26-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF p. 28, "home lending pricing (down 4 basis points)"), while the presentation splits it into home-loan competition -3bp and cash-rate lag -1bp (CBA-FY26-results-presentation.pdf, PDF p. 60, "Home loan competition (3)" and "Impact of cash rate lag (1)").

## Limitations and overall confidence

Overall confidence: **97/100**.

- The bridge is CBA's rounded Group disclosure; smaller offsets within categories may be hidden.

- The headline measure includes low-margin liquid assets and repos. CBA notes that rebased underlying FY25 NIM excludes a 4bp current-period mix impact from these balances (CBA-FY26-results-presentation.pdf, PDF p. 130, "Rebased underlying FY25 NIM excludes 4 basis point mix impact").

- Divisional NIMs have different perimeters and are not substituted into the Group bridge.

- Confidence scores and the residual calculation are analyst assessments; all operating figures and driver contributions come only from the four specified local PDFs.
