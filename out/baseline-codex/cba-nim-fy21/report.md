# Commonwealth Bank of Australia: FY21 NIM attribution

## Conclusion

CBA's Group net interest margin fell **4 basis points to 2.03% in FY21 from 2.07% in FY20**. The movement and comparator are disclosed directly in the FY21 profit announcement: “Net interest margin (%) 2.03 2.07 (4)bpts” (CBA-FY21-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF page 32, “Net interest margin (%) 2.03 2.07 (4)bpts”). The FY20 source independently reports the same 2.07% starting point (CBA-FY20-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF page 32, “decrease in net interest margin to 2.07%”).

The main negative was higher liquids at **-4 bp**. Lower deposit earnings and other funding effects were a net **-3 bp**, while asset pricing and capital/other each cost **-2 bp**. Offsets were basis risk at **+3 bp**, portfolio mix at **+2 bp**, and Treasury and Markets at **+2 bp**. These disclosed categories sum exactly to the reported **-4 bp** movement, leaving a **0 bp residual** on the rounded disclosed basis.

## Basis and comparator

I use **Group NIM for the full year ended 30 June 2021 versus the full year ended 30 June 2020**, on a **continuing-operations basis**, associated with CBA's **cash-basis net interest income** presentation. The table labels net interest income “cash basis” (CBA-FY21-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF page 32, “Net interest income - ‘cash basis’”), its footnote says average interest-earning assets are on “a continuing operations basis” (CBA-FY21-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF page 32, “Average interest earning assets is presented on a continuing operations basis”), and the presentation's Group-margin bridge is explicitly continuing operations (CBA-FY21-results-presentation.pdf, PDF page 63, “Presented on a continuing operations basis.”).

This is not a statutory-NPAT comparison. CBA does not provide a separate statutory-NIM bridge in the supplied documents.

## Reconciliation

The disclosed bridge reconciles without forcing:

**-4 bp liquids - 2 bp asset pricing - 3 bp deposit pricing/funding + 2 bp portfolio mix + 3 bp basis risk - 2 bp capital/other + 2 bp Treasury and Markets = -4 bp reported movement.**

The bridge values are visible together in CBA's full-year chart (CBA-FY21-results-presentation.pdf, PDF page 63, “FY20 207 ... FY21 203” and “(4)bpts”). **Residual: 0 bp**, calculated from CBA's rounded whole-basis-point driver disclosures.

## Source agreement

No numerical disagreement was found. The FY21 profit announcement, FY21 results presentation and FY20 profit announcement all support a **2.07% FY20** starting margin, a **2.03% FY21** ending margin and a **4 bp decline** (CBA-FY21-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF page 32, “2.03 2.07 (4)bpts”; CBA-FY21-results-presentation.pdf, PDF page 63, “FY20 207 ... FY21 203” and “(4)bpts”; CBA-FY20-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF page 32, “net interest margin to 2.07%”).

The presentation separates the deposit/funding subcomponents more finely than the profit announcement, but it does not conflict: its **-8 bp** cash-rate effect plus **+1 bp** investment-deposit effect equals the announcement's net **-7 bp** deposit effect, while both show **+2 bp** replicating-portfolio and **+2 bp** wholesale offsets (CBA-FY21-results-presentation.pdf, PDF page 63, “Impact of cash rate cut (8) Investment +1 R. Portfolio +2 Wholesale +2”; CBA-FY21-profit-announcement.pdf, PDF page 32, “decreases in the cash rate (down 7 basis points)”).

## Limitations and overall confidence

- The attribution is CBA's management bridge, not an independently estimated sensitivity. Effects are rounded to whole basis points and can embed interactions.

- The supplied sources do not disclose a separate statutory-NIM bridge.

- The **0 bp** residual is valid on the bank's rounded disclosed basis; it should not be interpreted as proof that there is no sub-basis-point rounding.

**Overall confidence: 99/100.** The headline movement, basis and every bridge category are directly disclosed and reconcile across the supplied sources.
