# CBA — nim — 1H26 vs 1H25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 204bps (-4bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin', column 31 Dec 24 -> column 31 Dec 25*

CBA's Group net interest margin decreased 4 basis points to 204 bps in 1H26 (ended Dec 2025) from 208 bps in 1H25 (ended Dec 2024). The decline was primarily driven by a 4 bps drag from higher liquid assets and institutional repos, alongside a 2 bps reduction in asset pricing due to competitive pressures in home and business lending. These were partially offset by a 6 bps benefit from the replicating portfolio and capital hedges, and a 1 bps positive contribution from favorable funding mix.
