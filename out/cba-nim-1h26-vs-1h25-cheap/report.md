# CBA — nim — 1H26 vs 1H25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 204bps (-4bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column 31 Dec 24 Half -> column 31 Dec 25 Half*

CBA's net interest margin decreased 4 basis points to 204 bps in 1H26 (Dec 2025) compared to 208 bps in 1H25 (Dec 2024). The decline was primarily driven by lower asset pricing (-2 bps), higher funding costs (-3 bps), and increased liquid assets drag (-3 bps). These negative factors were partially offset by a favorable portfolio mix (+1 bps) and significantly higher earnings from the replicating portfolio and capital hedges (+6 bps). Treasury & Markets contributed a further -3 bps drag.
