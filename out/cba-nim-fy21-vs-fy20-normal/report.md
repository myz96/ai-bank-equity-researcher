# CBA — nim — FY21 vs FY20

CBA Group net interest margin fell 4 bps in FY21, from 207 bps (2.07%) in FY20 to 203 bps (2.03%), per the profit announcement KPI table and stated narrative. The fall was driven by higher liquid asset balances (-4 bps), deposit pricing and funding (-3 bps), asset pricing competition (-2 bps) and capital/other (-2 bps), partly offset by basis risk including replicating portfolio (+3 bps), Treasury and Markets (+2 bps) and favourable portfolio mix (+2 bps). Note the half-on-half move was +3 bps to 2.04%, a different comparison. The Group margin row carries no basis label and is reported on the primary basis.
