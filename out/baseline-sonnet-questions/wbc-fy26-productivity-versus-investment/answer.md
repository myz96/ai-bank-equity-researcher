# WBC — Does FY26 targeted productivity restore positive jaws?

**Judgment: mainly an offset, not a jaws fix.** Neither document uses the word
"jaws," gives FY26 revenue/income growth guidance, or a CTI target — so no
disclosed jaws forecast exists. But the expense-side arithmetic argues against
productivity being sufficient on its own.

## FY25 expense bridge ($m, Group)

| Item | $m | Driver disclosed |
|---|---|---|
| FY24 base | 10,944 | — |
| Staff costs | +397 | Salary/EBA, super, investment in bankers |
| Technology | +146 | — |
| Volume and other | +199 | — |
| **Productivity** | **(402)** | Simpler operating model, reduced property footprint, digitisation |
| Investments | +359 | UNITE investment $399m, amortisation +$106m, partly offset by lower ex-UNITE spend |
| Restructuring charge | +273 | Fit for Growth |
| **FY25** | **11,916** | +9% (+6% ex restructuring) |

Net operating income grew only 3% (WBC-FY25-results-announcement.pdf, p.9), so on the
disclosed % movements, FY25 jaws (income − expense growth, not a WBC term) computes to
roughly **−6ppts** (−3ppts ex the one-off restructuring charge). The announcement states
outright: "Productivity provided a partial offset" (p.9) — FY25's $402m productivity
gain did not offset the period's cost growth.

## Investment mix and UNITE's profile

FY25 investment spend was $1,918m (+9%), split UNITE $660m (34%, "large" increase from
$147m), Growth & productivity $563m (30%), Risk & regulatory $695m (36%, −34%)
(results-announcement.pdf, p.19). UNITE's expensed share is disclosed at **74%** in the
announcement (p.19) versus **76%** in the presentation (p.15) — a genuine, unreconciled
discrepancy; the announcement is preferred as the primary lodged disclosure. FY26 UNITE
spend is guided to $850–950m, ~75% expensed (both docs); FY27–28 UNITE is ~40% of a
~$2bn p.a. Group investment envelope; FY29 spend is guided lower; the program "extends
into FY29" (presentation, p.14). Cumulative UNITE spend to Sep-25 was $807m, ~40% of
total planned FY25–1H29 investment (p.7) — arithmetically implying a total program cost
near $2.0bn, though WBC never states that total directly. Average capitalised-software
amortisation period shortened to 2.7 years from 3.1 years (results-announcement.pdf,
p.20), meaning capitalised UNITE spend will hit the P&L faster than before.

## Why productivity looks like an offset, not a fix

FY26 UNITE expensed cost (≈$638–712m at 75% of $850–950m) rises roughly $135–225m
above FY25's actual expensed UNITE cost (≈$488–502m, using the 74–76% range) — on its
own equal to roughly a third to nearly half of the entire ">$500m" FY26 productivity
target (presentation, p.27). Layer on FY26 EBA increases of 3–4% against a $6,326m FY25
staff base (results-announcement.pdf, p.18) and continuing "investment in bankers," and
the identified cost pressures plausibly approach or exceed the incremental productivity
gain (>$500m target vs. $402m realised in FY25 — an increase of just over $100m). This
mirrors FY25's own pattern, where a larger productivity gain than FY26's floor still
only "partially offset" cost growth.

## Limitations

- WBC gives no FY26 revenue, NIM, or CTI guidance — a jaws forecast cannot be computed, only inferred from expense dynamics.
- UNITE expensed % for FY25 conflicts between documents (74% vs 76%).
- Total UNITE program cost (~$2.0bn) is derived, not printed.
- Whether the $273m FY25 restructuring charge recurs in FY26 is not disclosed.
- The >$500m productivity target's composition (Fit for Growth vs. UNITE initiative benefits) is not itemised.

**Confidence: 62/100.**
