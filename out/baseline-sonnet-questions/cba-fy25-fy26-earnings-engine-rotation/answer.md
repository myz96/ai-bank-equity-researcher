## The rotation

FY25's cash-NPAT growth (+4%) was margin- and provision-led: NIM rose 9bps, mostly from replicating-portfolio and capital-hedge earnings (+9bps) and a liquids mix benefit (+7bps), while opex (+6%) outgrew income (+5%), holding pre-provision profit growth to +3%. The bottom line was then flattered by a shrinking impairment charge (−9%, "reflecting an improving base case economic outlook and lower loss experience").

FY26 rotates to a volume-led model. Average interest-earning assets rose 8% ($92bn), lifting NII +7% even as NIM fell 3bps to 2.05%. CBA's margin bridge (results presentation p.60) attributes the fall to Liquids & repos (−4bps) and Asset pricing (−5bps: home loan competition −3bps, cash-rate lag −1bp, business/institutional lending −1bp), partly offset by hedge income (+5bps) and Portfolio mix (+2bps, "deposits growing faster than lending"). Opex grew +6%, matching income, so pre-provision profit growth doubled to +6% and cost-to-income improved 20bps to 45.5%. Loan impairment expense rose +9% (loss rate 7bps→8bps) on "portfolio growth, cost-of-living pressures and increased geopolitical risk" — provisioning for risk, not realised losses, which the ASX announcement states "remained low," with provision coverage steady at 1.53% of credit RWA and the loss rate still below its 17-year historical range (results presentation p.69).

## Judgment

On the disclosed evidence, FY26's faster cash-NPAT growth (+7% vs +4%) is higher quality. It is broader-based (income growth matched cost growth for the first time in this comparison, doubling pre-provision profit growth to +6%) and it absorbed, rather than depended on, both NIM compression and a rising loan-loss rate — whereas FY25's growth relied on non-repeatable margin tailwinds and a shrinking impairment charge while pre-provision profit growth lagged at +3%.

## Limitations

- No published dollar bridge decomposes cash-NPAT growth into volume/margin/cost/impairment/tax; this judgment is inferred from growth-rate comparisons and the bps-level NIM bridge.
- Other operating income includes unquantified one-off items (CommInsure milestone payment, Gemini IPO gain), so "clean" organic OOI growth cannot be isolated.
- The higher loan-loss rate is expected-loss provisioning; documents assert realised losses "remained low" but disclose no write-off rate to verify this.

**Confidence: 78/100**
