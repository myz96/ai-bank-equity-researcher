# CBA — cash_earnings — FY21 vs FY20


CBA's cash NPAT from continuing operations rose $1,428m or 19.8% to $8,653m in FY21 (FY20: $7,225m). The bank attributes the result to a 1.7% increase in operating income, a 3.3% increase in operating expenses and a $1,964m decrease in loan impairment expense. Statutory NPAT from continuing operations rose $1,455m or 19.7% to $8,843m; the $190m gap to cash NPAT reflects non-cash items (gain on disposals net of transaction costs $183m vs $70m, and hedging & IFRS volatility $7m vs $93m). The bridge: NII +$229m, other operating income +$166m, underlying operating expenses -$249m, remediation/notable items -$114m, loan impairment +$1,964m, and tax -$568m, summing to +$1,428m with no residual.
