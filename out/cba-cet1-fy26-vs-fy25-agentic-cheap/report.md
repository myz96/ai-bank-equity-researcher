# CBA — cet1 — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 1230bps → 1200bps (-30bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 65/100

*Read from: row 'Common Equity Tier 1 (APRA) (%)', column 30 Jun 25 column -> column 30 Jun 26 column*

CBA's APRA Level 2 CET1 ratio fell 30 bps to 12.0% in FY26 from 12.3% in FY25. The decline was driven by full-year dividends (final $2.70 + interim $2.70 per share, totalling $8.3bn including DRP buybacks) which reduced CET1, partly offset by capital generated from earnings (cash NPAT of $11.0bn, up 7% on FY25). Higher Credit Risk RWA (+$23.8bn to $422.7bn) and IRRBB RWA (+$1.8bn to $41.7bn) diluted the ratio, partially offset by lower Traded Market Risk RWA (-$2.7bn to $7.0bn). Operational Risk RWA rose to $51.0bn. No buy-back activity occurred in FY26. Excluding the interim dividend payment, CET1 increased 52 bps in H1, demonstrating strong organic capital generation.
