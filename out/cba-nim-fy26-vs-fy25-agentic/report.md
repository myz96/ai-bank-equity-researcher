# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 93/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column Full Year Ended 30 Jun 25 -> column Full Year Ended 30 Jun 26*

CBA's Group net interest margin fell 3bps to 2.05% in FY26 from 2.08% in FY25 (ev-9, ev-18, ev-41). Net interest income still rose $1,563m or 7% to $25,586m as average interest earning assets grew 8% (ev-16, ev-11). The bank frames the decline as essentially technical: "Margins were broadly stable excluding growth in liquid assets and institutional reverse sale and repurchase agreements, which have broadly neutral impacts on net interest income" (ev-39, ev-11, ev-42). The results book walk (primary framing) is: Liquids -3, Asset pricing -5, Funding costs 0, Portfolio mix +2, Basis risk 0, Capital/Replicating/Other +5, Treasury & Markets -2 (ev-1). So competition-driven lending margin compression (-5bps, mostly home loans -4bps) and liquids drag (-3bps) were largely offset by hedge/replicating earnings (+5bps) and favourable asset and funding mix (+2bps). The slide deck publishes the same 208→205 walk with a different cut of two bars (Liquids & repos -4, Treasury & Markets -1) — a definitional grouping difference, not a different total (ev-21).

## Limitations
- The bank qualifies the headline decline heavily: excluding growth in liquid assets and institutional reverse repos - which have 'broadly neutral impacts on net interest income' - it says margin was stable (ev-11, ev-39). On that basis the -3bps liquids bar (and the repo component of Treasury & Markets) does not reduce net interest income dollars, so the reported NIM fall overstates the economic margin pressure.
- 'Funding costs: Flat.' and 'Basis risk: Flat.' are published as zero bars with no explanation of what offset within them; the deposit/wholesale split inside funding costs is not disclosed for the full year. Deposit growth benefit is captured under Portfolio mix instead, so funding.deposits cannot be quantified separately.
- The half-on-half walk (Dec 25 to Jun 26: Funding costs +2, Asset pricing -5, Capital/Replicating +3, Treasury & Markets +1) is a different comparison and was excluded from the driver table.
- No CBA-published 'NIM excluding liquids and pooled facilities' numeric level was found; the 'broadly stable' claim is narrative only, not a quantified alternative margin.
