## Limitations
- The results book and the presentation publish different framings of the same walk: the book shows Liquids −3 and Treasury & Markets −2; the slide shows combined Liquids & repos −4 (liquid assets −3, institutional repos −1) and Treasury & Markets −1. Both sum to −3; the book framing is adopted per results-book primacy.
- The −3 bps liquids bar is a denominator effect: the bank states liquid assets and institutional reverse sale and repurchase agreements 'have broadly neutral impacts on net interest income' and that NIM excluding their growth was stable. The underlying margin decline is therefore smaller than the headline −3 bps.
- The slide's asset-pricing sub-split (home loan competition −3, cash rate lag −1, business & insto. lending −1) is slide-level annotation and does not reconcile line-for-line with the book's home lending −4 / business and institutional −1 split.
- The Group NIM row carries no cash or statutory label; it is reported here on the bank's primary (cash) basis per the KPI table footnote 'Presented on a cash basis unless stated otherwise', on a continuing-operations basis in the NII table.
- The presentation's zero Funding costs bar is printed as a dash ('-') in the chart text layer; its zero value is read from the chart image (ev-12), not from a printed numeral.
