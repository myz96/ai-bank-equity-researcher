# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 93/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column Full Year Ended 30 Jun 25 -> column Full Year Ended 30 Jun 26*

CBA's Group net interest margin fell 3bpts to 2.05% in FY26 from 2.08% in FY25 (KPI table, cash basis; FY25 book prints the same 2.08%). Net interest income still rose $1,563m or 7% to $25,586m, driven by an 8% ($92bn) rise in average interest earning assets to $1,246bn, "partly offset by a 3 basis point decrease in net interest margin". The bank frames the decline as essentially technical: "Excluding growth in liquid assets, which have broadly neutral impacts on net interest income, net interest margin was stable" — average liquid assets grew to $187,337m from $172,050m. Underlying, lower lending margins from competition (-5bpts) were offset by replicating-portfolio and capital hedge earnings (+5bpts) and favourable business-lending/deposit mix (+2bpts), with liquids (-3bpts) and Treasury & Markets (-2bpts) taking the margin down. The slide walk (p60) publishes the same 208→205 with slightly different bar cuts (Liquids & repos -4, Treasury & Markets -1).

## Limitations
- The bank qualifies the headline decline heavily: it says margins were 'broadly stable' excluding liquid asset and institutional repo growth, which have 'broadly neutral impacts on net interest income' - so the -3bpt reported fall overstates the economic margin pressure, and the liquids/repo bars carry 'Minimal impact on earnings'.
- The two published walks for FY25->FY26 allocate the repo drag differently (liquids -3/T&M -2 in the book vs liquids&repos -4/T&M -1 on the slide); the results book framing is adopted and the slide bars are not mixed in.
- The 'Funding costs: Flat' and 'Basis risk: Flat' bars are net zeros with no gross components disclosed for the full year; the half-on-half commentary (funding +2bpts from rising rates partly offset by unfavourable deposit mix) belongs to the Dec 25 -> Jun 26 half comparison and is not used here.
- Sub-splits inside asset pricing differ between documents (book: home lending -4, business/institutional -1; slide: home loan competition -3, cash rate lag -1, business & insto -1). Both total -5.
