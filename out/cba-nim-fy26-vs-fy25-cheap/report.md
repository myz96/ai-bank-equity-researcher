# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 95/100

CBA's Group Net Interest Margin (NIM) decreased by 3 basis points to 205 bps in FY26 (FY25: 208 bps). The decline was primarily driven by a 5 bps headwind from asset pricing pressure and a 4 bps drag from growth in liquid assets and repos. These were partially offset by a 5 bps benefit from capital/replicating portfolio earnings and a 2 bps tailwind from favorable portfolio mix. Funding costs and basis risk remained flat.
