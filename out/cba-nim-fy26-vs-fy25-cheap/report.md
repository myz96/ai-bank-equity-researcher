*Read from: row 'Net interest margin', column FY25 -> column FY26*

CBA's Group NIM decreased by 3 bps to 205 bps in FY26 (from 208 bps in FY25). The decline was driven primarily by lower asset pricing (-5 bps) and higher liquid assets/repo balances (-4 bps), partially offset by favourable portfolio mix (+2 bps) and higher capital/replicating earnings (+5 bps).
