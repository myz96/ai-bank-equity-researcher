# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 100/100

These were partially offset by a 5 bps tailwind from capital/replicating portfolios and a 2 bps benefit from portfolio mix.
