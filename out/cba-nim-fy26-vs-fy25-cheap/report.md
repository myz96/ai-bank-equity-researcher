# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin', column FY25 -> column FY26*

CBA's Group net interest margin decreased by 3 bps to 205 bps in FY26 (vs 208 bps in FY25). The decline was driven primarily by lower asset pricing (-5 bps) and higher liquid assets drag (-4 bps), partially offset by favourable capital replicating earnings (+5 bps) and portfolio mix (+2 bps). Funding costs were neutral.
