# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin', column FY25 Total Group -> column FY26 Total Group*

CBA's cash net interest margin decreased by 3 basis points to 205 bps in FY26 (from 208 bps in FY25). The decline was primarily driven by lower asset pricing (-5 bps) and the drag from higher liquid assets and repos (-4 bps), partially offset by favourable portfolio mix (+2 bps) and higher earnings on capital/replicating portfolios (+5 bps). Funding costs and basis risk were flat.
