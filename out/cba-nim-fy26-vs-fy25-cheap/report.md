# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin', column FY25 -> column FY26*

CBA's Group net interest margin decreased by 3 basis points to 205 bps in FY26 (from 208 bps in FY25). The decline was primarily driven by lower asset pricing (-5 bps) and higher liquid assets/repos drag (-4 bps), partially offset by favourable portfolio mix (+2 bps) and higher capital/replicating earnings (+5 bps). Funding costs were flat.
