# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

CBA's cash NIM declined by 3 bps to 205 bps in FY26 (FY25: 208 bps). The decline was driven primarily by asset pricing headwinds (-5 bps) and increased liquid assets/repo drag (-4 bps), partially offset by positive contributions from capital/replicating portfolio earnings (+5 bps) and favorable portfolio mix (+2 bps). Funding costs were flat year-on-year.
