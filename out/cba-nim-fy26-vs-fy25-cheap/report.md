# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 40/100

CBA's cash NIM decreased by 3 bps to 205 bps in FY26 (FY25: 208 bps). The decline was primarily driven by lower asset pricing (-5 bps) due to competitive pressure on home and business lending margins, and a drag from growth in liquid assets and repos (-3 bps). These were partially offset by favourable portfolio mix (+2 bps) from strong business lending and deposit growth, and higher earnings on the replicating portfolio and capital hedges (+5 bps). Funding costs and basis risk had no net impact.
