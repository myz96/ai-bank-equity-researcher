# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 95/100

CBA's cash NIM decreased by 3 bps to 205 bps in FY26 (FY25: 208 bps). The decline was driven primarily by asset pricing pressures (-5 bps) and the drag from growth in liquid assets and repos (-4 bps), partially offset by favourable portfolio mix (+2 bps) and capital/replicating earnings (+5 bps). Funding costs were flat.
