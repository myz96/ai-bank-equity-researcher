# CBA — nim — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 208bps → 205bps (-3bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 90/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin', column FY25 -> column FY26*

CBA's Group NIM decreased by 3 bps to 205 bps in FY26 (from 208 bps in FY25). The decline was driven by a 5 bps negative impact from asset pricing competition and a 4 bps drag from increased liquid assets and institutional repos. These were partially offset by a 5 bps positive contribution from capital replicating portfolio earnings and a 2 bps benefit from favorable portfolio mix. Funding costs and basis risk were flat.
