# CBA — nim — FY25 vs FY24

**Movement (cash basis):** 199bps → 208bps (+9bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 93/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column 30 Jun 24 (Full Year Ended) -> column 30 Jun 25 (Full Year Ended)*

CBA's Group net interest margin rose 9bps to 2.08% in FY25 from 1.99% in FY24 (KPI table, cash basis; same row and change also printed in the Net Interest Income note and the NIM appendix, where Group interest spread widened to 1.46% from 1.40% and the interest-free liabilities/equity benefit to 0.62% from 0.59%). Most of the gain is optical: a 7bps benefit from a reduction in lower yielding liquid assets (5bps; average liquid assets down $27bn or 14% after RBA TFF repayment) and institutional pooled facilities (2bps), which the bank says has "a broadly neutral impact on net interest income". Excluding that mix effect, margin rose only 2bps, as higher capital and replicating-portfolio hedge earnings (+9bps) more than offset a 7bps drag from increased deposit price competition. Asset pricing and portfolio mix were flat, basis risk -1bp, Treasury and Markets +1bp. NII rose $1,199m or 5% to $24,023m.

## Limitations
- Only the FY25 profit announcement publishes the FY24->FY25 NIM walk; the FY24 results presentation's Group margin walk covers FY23->FY24 (a different comparison) and so cannot corroborate the bar values. Corroboration of the 9bps movement rests on three separate tables/notes within the FY25 book plus the FY24 book's 1.99% base.
- The Funding costs bar is a net number; CBA attributes it to increased deposit price competition but does not split deposits from wholesale funding for the full year.
- The bank does not disclose reasons behind the zero Asset pricing and Portfolio mix bars, nor behind the +1bp Treasury and Markets bar.
