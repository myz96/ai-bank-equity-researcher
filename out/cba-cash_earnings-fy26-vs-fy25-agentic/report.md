## Limitations
- The bank publishes no single cash-NPAT waterfall for FY26 vs FY25; the bridge is assembled from the Group Performance Summary table (ev-1 to ev-7) plus each component's own stated 'on the prior year' movement. Every component is a bank-stated movement or a bank-published primary walk bar, and the six components sum exactly to the $730m movement, leaving a zero residual.
- NII is claimed as a single total. CBA does not split the $1,563m into dollar volume and margin effects; the volume (+$92bn AIEA) and margin (-3bpts) drivers are disclosed only in units, so nii.margin is carried as an unquantified-in-dollars driver with its bps detail.
- The NIM walk on p28 (Liquids -0.02, Asset pricing -0.05, Funding costs 0.00, Portfolio mix +0.02, Basis risk 0.00, Capital/Replicating and Other, Treasury & Markets) is printed in percentage points, not dollars, and its bars could not be paired to labels via the chart reader; the bps figures used are those CBA states in the accompanying narrative text.
- CBA qualifies the NIM move: liquid-asset growth has 'broadly neutral impacts on net interest income' and ex-liquids NIM was 'stable' (ev-20), so the headline -3bpts overstates the economic margin pressure on the NII bar.
- The $170m FY26 notable items figure is not broken into per-item amounts (NZ legal settlement, ASIC Better Banking goodwill payment, domestic remediation); only the FY25 $130m is itemised ($52m/$33m/$45m). The $40m delta is therefore attributable in total but not by sub-item.
- The tax contribution is the movement in tax EXPENSE (-$208m). It nets a volume effect (higher pre-tax profit) against a favourable 50bpt fall in the effective rate; CBA does not disclose the dollar split between the two.
- The productivity bar (-$404m) in the expense walk is a net offset within a gross expense increase; CBA also cites 'favourable FX' as an offset across staff, occupancy and other expenses without quantifying it.
- All figures are continuing-operations basis. Cash NPAT including discontinued operations is $10,982m FY26 vs $10,253m FY25 ($1m of FY25 discontinued cash profit), a $729m movement — immaterially different from the $730m continuing-operations bridge.
- Capped at 80: notable_items -40 $m. The records these claims cite do not state those numbers, so each one is arithmetic over the evidence rather than a figure read from it.
- Expenses are claimed on the underlying/notable split; the bank equally publishes the combined headline framing, so both claims are capped at 80.
