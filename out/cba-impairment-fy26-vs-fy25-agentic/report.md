CBA's loan impairment expense (LIE) rose $62m or 9% to $788m in FY26 from $726m in FY25 (continuing operations, cash basis), still below FY24's $802m; the presentation prints the same movement as +8.5% (rounding). The loss rate rose 1bp to 8bps of average gross loans and acceptances (GLAA) — consumer 5→6bps, corporate 14→12bps. The bank's own provision-type bridge in the impairment note explains the whole $62m: net collective provision funding +$150m (456→606), partly offset by lower net new and increased individually assessed provisioning -$17m (439→422) and larger write-backs of individually assessed provisions -$71m (169→240). By division: Retail +$106m to $378m and New Zealand +$11m to $66m, partly offset by Business Banking -$45m to $310m and IB&M -$16m to $33m (Corporate Centre +$6m to $1m). Total provisions rose only $99m or 2% to $6,476m; CBA describes coverage as "Strong provision coverage maintained".

## Limitations
- CBA publishes no waterfall/bridge chart for the impairment charge movement; the quantified bridge is built from the provision-type rows of note 2.2 (collective funding, new/increased individual provisioning, write-backs), which sum exactly to the +$62m movement with zero residual.
- The +$150m collective provision funding increase is not disaggregated by the bank into volume growth versus risk migration versus forward-looking adjustments/overlays, so collective.volume and overlays_fla cannot be quantified for the FY26 vs FY25 comparison.
- No FY26-vs-FY25 quantification of overlay/FLA releases: the bank only quantifies overlay releases half-on-half (Retail -$86m 2H26 vs 1H26 from "release of overlays raised in prior periods as higher risk customer cohorts performed better than expected"), a different comparison, and mentions "more targeted forward-looking adjustments" only against provision balances.
- Provision balance movements (corporate collective +$172m to $2,797m; consumer collective -$48m to $2,888m) are provision-stock deltas, used as narrative context only, not as bridge components.
- New Zealand's +$18m impairment movement on slide 56 is in NZD; the AUD movement in the results book is +$11m (66 vs 55).
- Corporate Centre and Other moved +$6m (from a $5m benefit to a $1m expense) and is included in the divisional where-layer but is not a provision type; it is absorbed within the provision-type bridge.
- Capped at 80: collective.asset_quality +150 $m, individual_provisions -17 $m, write_backs_recoveries -71 $m. The records these claims cite do not state those numbers, so each one is arithmetic over the evidence rather than a figure read from it.
