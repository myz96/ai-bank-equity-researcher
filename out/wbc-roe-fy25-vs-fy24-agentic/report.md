# WBC — roe — FY25 vs FY24

**Movement (ex_notables basis):** 11.21ppt → 10.97ppt (-0.24ppt) | **Attribution confidence:** 76/100

*Read from: row 'ROTE (excluding Notable Items)', column Full Year Sept 2024 -> column Full Year Sept 2025*

Westpac's headline ROTE ex Notable Items fell 24bps, from 11.21% in FY24 to 10.97% in FY25 (ev-1, ev-18), corroborated by the pack's "11.0% ROTE ex Notable Items, 24bps to FY24" (ev-14, ev-26). The fall is almost entirely an earnings effect: the ROTE numerator, net profit attributable to owners adjusted for RSP dividends ex Notable Items, fell $140m to $6,966m from $7,106m (ev-16), with net profit ex Notable Items down 2% to $6,972m as higher income and lower impairment charges were "more than offset by higher expenses" (ev-8, ev-9). Average tangible ordinary equity was essentially flat at $63,476m versus $63,415m (+$61m) (ev-17), so the denominator added only ~2bps of drag. On the statutory basis the decline is smaller: statutory ROTE fell 12bps, 11.01% to 10.89% (ev-2), because Notable Item hedging losses narrowed from $123m to $56m (ev-13).

## Limitations
- Westpac publishes no ROTE walk or bridge chart for FY24 to FY25 in either document. Both driver contributions are my own arithmetic derivation from the ROTE numerator and denominator printed on results announcement p58, not a bank decomposition, so both are capped below chart-level confidence.
- The equity_effect value bundles the pure denominator effect and the earnings/equity interaction term; the bank does not split these.
- The earnings walk on pack p40 (primary, FY24 to FY25, sum check passed) decomposes net profit ex Notable Items of $7,113m to $6,972m, not the RSP-adjusted ROTE numerator of $7,106m to $6,966m. The $7m/$6m RSP dividend adjustments (ev-16) mean the walk's bars explain the earnings effect qualitatively but do not tie exactly to the ROTE numerator.
- The bank qualifies the expense increase: excluding the $273m restructuring charge expenses rose 6%, with "Productivity provided a partial offset" (ev-10) - so the gross cost pressure on ROTE is larger than the net 9% line implies.
- Pack p31's CET1 walk is a Mar-25 to Sep-25 (half-on-half) framing and was not used for any contribution.
- Buyback and DRP evidence (ev-29, ev-30, ev-31) supports the direction of the equity effect qualitatively; the bank does not quantify the buyback's or DRP's impact on average tangible equity or on ROTE.
