# CBA — cet1 — FY26 vs FY25

**Movement (cash basis):** 1230bps → 1200bps (-30bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 40/100

*Read from: row 'Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) (APRA Level 2)', column 30 Jun 25 -> column 30 Jun 26*

CBA's APRA Level 2 CET1 ratio fell 30bpts to 12.0% at 30 June 2026 from 12.3% at 30 June 2025, still well above APRA's 10.25% minimum (ev-1, ev-44, ev-52). The ratio was squeezed because RWA grew faster than capital: total RWA rose 5% to $522,407m from $496,145m (ev-19, ev-45) while CET1 capital rose only $1,795m to $62,762m (ev-26). Credit risk RWA rose 6% to $422,703m (ev-46) — the bank states it "deployed 72bpts of capital into credit risk weighted assets" across FY26 on volume growth in commercial portfolios and domestic mortgages (ev-53). Offsetting: cash NPAT of $10,982m, up 7% (ev-48), against a $5.05 fully-franked DPS with a 77% payout ratio and a fully-neutralised DRP (ev-47, ev-56). Level 1 CET1 fell 30bpts to 12.1% and the internationally comparable ratio 40bpts to 18.3% (ev-17, ev-18) — context measures only. CBA publishes no FY26-vs-FY25 CET1 walk; only a Dec 25→Jun 26 walk (ev-6).

## Limitations
- CBA publishes NO FY26-vs-FY25 CET1 walk. Both the results book (p48) and the presentation (p32, p106) show only the Dec 25 -> Jun 26 half-on-half walk, whose own driver list is explicitly scoped 'for the 6 months ended 30 June 2026'. Its bars (-76 dividend, +106 Cash NPAT, -46 RWA, -8 Other) are reported inside narratives as context for that half only, never as FY-on-FY contributions.
- Only one driver is quantified on the task comparison: the 72bpts of capital deployed into credit RWA (ev-53). All other drivers carry contribution null, so the quantified drivers do not sum to the -30bpts movement; the residual of +42bpts is the unquantified net of earnings, dividends, other RWA lines and regulatory adjustments and must not be attributed to any one driver.
- The 72bpts credit-RWA figure appears only in the ASX/media release, the weakest of the FY26 sources under the hierarchy. It is directionally corroborated by the results-book credit RWA table (+6% to $422,703m, ev-46, ev-20) but no results-book page restates it in bpts, so it is capped below 90.
- Full-year bpts impacts are not disclosed for dividends net of DRP, cash NPAT, IRRBB, operational risk, traded market risk or Other. Where I state $m movements for these I computed them by subtracting the 30 Jun 25 column from the 30 Jun 26 column of the appendix tables; the framing is mine, not CBA's, and no bps conversion is asserted.
- The APS 117 relief of -$10bn is disclosed in RWA dollars only, not in CET1 bpts, and its Jun 25 base is on the pre-1 October 2025 framework (ev-59), so Jun 25 and Jun 26 IRRBB RWA are not measured on a like-for-like basis.
- The RWA bar in CBA's walk excludes FX, which is carried inside 'Other' along with intangibles, associate profits/losses and reserve movements (ev-13, ev-14). The RWA and Other drivers are therefore not cleanly separable, and Cash NPAT excludes equity-accounted associate results as capital neutral (ev-12).
- Operational risk RWA is flat half-on-half but up $3,417m year-on-year purely through the APS 115 three-year net-income averaging reset; CBA states no CET1 bpts cost for this, so the driver is unquantified.
- The divestments driver is recorded as a zero contribution because the FY26 walk publishes no divestment bar. This is my inference from the bar's absence plus the FY25 walk's +20bpts bar, not a statement CBA makes about FY26.
- Capped at 80: capital_returns +0 bps. The records these claims cite do not state those numbers, so each one is arithmetic over the evidence rather than a figure read from it.
