# WBC — cet1 — FY25 vs FY24

Westpac's APRA Level 2 CET1 capital ratio rose 4 bps to 12.53% at 30 September 2025 from 12.49% at 30 September 2024. The increase was driven by net profit accumulation and other reserve movements (including lower capitalised software balances), partially offset by higher total RWA ($12.6 billion increase, mainly from IRRBB RWA) and on-market share buybacks. The internationally comparable CET1 ratio moved 1 bps to 18.28%. No full-year CET1 walk chart is published; the bank provides only a half-on-half (Mar-25 to Sep-25) walk showing +80 bps from net profit, −58 bps from dividends, −7 bps from RWA, +16 bps from capital deductions/other items, and −2 bps from capital return.
