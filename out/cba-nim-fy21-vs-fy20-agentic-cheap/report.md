# CBA — nim — FY21 vs FY20

**Movement (cash basis):** 207bps → 203bps (-4bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 88/100

*Read from: row 'Total Group Net interest margin', column Full Year ended 30 Jun 20 -> column Full Year ended 30 Jun 21*

CBA's Group net interest margin declined 4 bps in FY21 to 2.03% (from 2.07% in FY20), driven by higher liquid assets drag (-4 bps), lower asset pricing (-2 bps), and lower deposit pricing and funding (-3 bps), partially offset by favourable portfolio mix (+2 bps), lower basis risk exposure (+3 bps), and higher Treasury & Markets contribution (+2 bps). The decline reflected the ongoing impact of a low interest rate environment, partly offset by management actions, lower wholesale funding costs and favourable funding mix.
