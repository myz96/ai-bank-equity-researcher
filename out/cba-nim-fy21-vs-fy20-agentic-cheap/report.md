# CBA — nim — FY21 vs FY20

**Movement (cash basis):** 207bps → 203bps (-4bps) | **Attribution confidence:** 88/100

*Read from: row 'Net interest margin (%)', column 30 Jun 20 -> column 30 Jun 21*

CBA's Group net interest margin (cash basis, continuing operations) fell 4 bps to 203 bps in FY21 from 207 bps in FY20. The decline was driven by higher liquid assets (-4 bps), weaker asset pricing (-2 bps) from home loan competition and consumer finance mix shift, and lower deposit margins (-3 bps) from cash rate cuts, partially offset by favourable portfolio mix (+2 bps) from higher deposit funding ratios, improved basis risk (+3 bps) from a narrower bill/OIS spread, and stronger Treasury & Markets contribution (+2 bps). Lower earnings on Group capital (-2 bps) also weighed.
