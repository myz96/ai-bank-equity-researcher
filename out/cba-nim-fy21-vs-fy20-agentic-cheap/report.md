CBA's net interest margin decreased 4 basis points year-on-year to 2.03% (from 2.07% in FY20), driven primarily by higher liquid asset drag (-4 bps), lower asset pricing (-2 bps), and reduced funding costs (-3 bps), partially offset by favourable portfolio mix (+2 bps), improved basis risk (+3 bps), and higher Treasury & Markets contribution (+2 bps).
