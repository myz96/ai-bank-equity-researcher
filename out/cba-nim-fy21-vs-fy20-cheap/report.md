CBA's Group net interest margin decreased 4 basis points to 203 bps in FY21 (from 207 bps in FY20). The decline was primarily driven by higher liquid assets (-4 bps) and lower asset pricing (-2 bps), partially offset by favourable portfolio mix (+2 bps) and basis risk (+3 bps).
