# NAB — cash_earnings — FY25 vs FY24

**Movement (cash basis):** 7102$m → 7091$m (-11$m) | **Attribution confidence:** 40/100

*Read from: row 'Cash earnings', column Year to Sep 24 ($m) -> column Year to Sep 25 ($m)*

NAB's FY25 cash earnings were essentially flat: $7,091m against $7,102m, a fall of $11 million or 0.2% (ev-1, ev-13, ev-70). The bank leads with total net operating income of $20,813m, up 2.9% from $20,236m — revenue growth "with key drivers including volume growth and higher Markets & Treasury (M&T) income", and only 1.4% ex-M&T (ev-4, ev-45). Operating performance (underlying profit, income less operating expenses before impairment) rose 1.3% to $10,965m from $10,823m (ev-6, ev-47, ev-77). Statutory net profit fell further, down $201m or 2.9% to $6,759m from $6,960m (ev-10, ev-13, ev-71), because non-cash items widened: acquisitions, disposals and business closures cost $295m after tax versus $4m (a $291m deterioration), partly offset by a $34m swing in hedging and fair value volatility to +$28m (ev-11, ev-72, ev-73). Revenue growth of +$577m was absorbed almost exactly by higher expenses (+$435m, headline), a higher impairment charge (+$105m) and higher tax and minorities (+$48m).

## Source disagreements
- **Cash vs statutory movement FY25 v FY24** (definitional): cash earnings -$11m / -0.2% vs statutory net profit -$201m / -2.9%
  Preferred: cash earnings -$11m. Cash earnings excludes non-cash items. The $190m wider statutory fall is driven by acquisitions, integration, disposals and business closures (-$295m after tax vs -$4m, a $291m deterioration), a $74m smaller discontinued-operations loss and a +$34m hedging/fair value swing (ev-72, ev-73, ev-71). NAB's primary measure is cash earnings.
- **Total NII movement: performance table vs volume/rate analysis** (definitional): $644m (Group performance results, cash basis) vs $646m (volume and rate analysis)
  Preferred: $644m. The Group performance results table prints a cash-basis NII increase of $644m (ev-14, ev-2); the average balance sheet volume and rate analysis, prepared on the statutory average balance sheet, totals $646m (ev-34). The $2m gap is a basis/scope difference, so the volume (+$1,204m) and rate (-$558m) split is used as a sub-decomposition only.
- **New Zealand Banking cash earnings FY25** (definitional): $1,353m, +1.5% (results book, AUD) vs $1,485m, +2.8% (presentation, local currency)
  Preferred: $1,353m. The results book divisional table reports New Zealand Banking in AUD ($1,353m, +1.5%) while presentation page 48 reports it in NZD local currency ($1,485m, +2.8%). Not used as a Group bridge driver either way.

## Limitations
- NAB publishes no cash earnings waterfall for FY25 v FY24. The bridge here is assembled from the Group performance results table's stated year-on-year movements (ev-13 to ev-21), each of which the bank states against the September 2024 comparative; the components sum exactly to the -$11m movement (+644 -67 -435 -105 -27 -21), so no residual arises.
- The operating expense contribution is split into UNDERLYING (-$305m, ex payroll review) and the payroll review/remediation notable (-$130m); these sum to the headline -$435m increase. The only primary walk chart (presentation p25) is an EXPENSE walk covering Sep 24 to Sep 25, not a cash earnings walk; its bars are reported inside the expense narrative rather than as separate cash earnings drivers.
- The NII volume (+$1,204m) and rate (-$558m) split comes from the statutory average balance sheet volume/rate analysis and totals $646m, not the $644m cash figure; it is a sub-decomposition of the nii driver and must not be added to it.
- The $74m economic hedge movement is a gross transfer between NII and other operating income and is revenue-neutral at Group level: NII's $644m increase includes a $74m decrease from hedges while other operating income's $67m fall includes a $74m increase (ev-14, ev-16). Underlying figures are $718m and -$8m respectively.
- The bank gives no explanation for the $21m increase in non-controlling interests (from $18m to $39m), which is folded into tax_and_other on arithmetic alone.
- Comparatives were restated during FY25 for the presentation of directly attributable income-earning expenses and for portfolio transfers between Personal Banking and Business & Private Banking (ev-5 footnotes), so FY24 figures here are restated ones.
- Capped at 80: tax_and_other -48 $m. The records these claims cite do not state those numbers, so each one is arithmetic over the evidence rather than a figure read from it.
