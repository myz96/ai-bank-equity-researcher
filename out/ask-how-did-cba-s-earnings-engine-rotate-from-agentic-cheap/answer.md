*CBA, periods FY26, FY25 — confidence 85/100*

CBA's earnings engine rotated from NIM-driven growth in FY25 to volume-driven growth in FY26. Cash NPAT grew 7% ($10,982m, up $730m) vs 4% ($10,252m, up $416m) in FY25. The higher LIE level reflects proactive provisioning for geopolitical risk and macro uncertainty, not deterioration in credit quality.
