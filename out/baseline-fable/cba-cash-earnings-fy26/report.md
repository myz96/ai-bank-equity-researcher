# CBA FY26 Cash Earnings Attribution

**Coverage:** Commonwealth Bank of Australia (ASX: CBA), full year ended 30 June 2026 versus full year ended 30 June 2025.
**Basis:** Cash net profit after tax (cash NPAT) from continuing operations. FY25 comparatives are restated to the current-period presentation. The restatement did not change the FY25 group cash NPAT of $10,252m.
**Sources:** FY26 Profit Announcement (PA26), FY26 Results Presentation (RP26), FY26 ASX Announcement (ASX26), FY25 Profit Announcement (PA25). Page references are PDF pages.
**Overall confidence: 96/100.**

## 1. The movement

Cash NPAT from continuing operations rose $730m, or 7%, from $10,252m in FY25 to $10,982m in FY26.

> "Cash net profit after tax (cash NPAT or cash profit) from continuing operations increased $730 million or 7% on the prior year to $10,982 million." (PA26, p26)

The cash P&L bridge (PA26, p16; all $m, FY26 vs FY25):

| Line | FY26 | FY25 | Impact on cash NPAT |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net interest income | 25,586 | 24,023 | +1,563 |
| Other operating income | 4,638 | 4,442 | +196 |
| Underlying operating expenses | (13,585) | (12,866) | (719) |
| Restructuring and notable items | (170) | (130) | (40) |
| Loan impairment expense | (788) | (726) | (62) |
| Corporate tax expense | (4,699) | (4,491) | (208) |
| **Cash NPAT (continuing operations)** | **10,982** | **10,252** | **+730** |

The six impacts sum to +$730m. **The residual is zero.** (Underlying and notable expense split: PA26, p27.)

## 2. Cash versus statutory

Statutory NPAT from continuing operations rose $778m, or 8%, from $10,133m to $10,911m (PA26, p26). Statutory NPAT with discontinued operations rose $750m, or 7%, from $10,116m to $10,866m (PA26, p16, p26).

Non-cash items explain the gap between the two bases (PA26, p17):

| Non-cash item (after tax, $m) | FY26 | FY25 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Loss on acquisition, disposal, closure, demerger (continuing) | (17) | (172) |
| Hedging and IFRS volatility | (54) | 53 |
| Continuing operations subtotal | (71) | (119) |
| Discontinued operations (CFS disposal costs) | (45) | (18) |
| **Total non-cash items** | **(116)** | **(137)** |

Statutory profit grew $48m faster than cash profit on the continuing basis. The FY25 statutory result carried larger disposal losses (Bank of Hangzhou and other divestments; PA26, p17 footnote). The FY26 statutory result carried a negative swing in hedging and IFRS volatility instead. RP26 (p23) shows the same reconciliation and calls statutory NPAT "broadly in line with Cash NPAT".

**Caution:** ASX26 (p1) headlines statutory NPAT of $10,911m (up 8%). That figure is the continuing-operations value. Total statutory NPAT, with the $45m CFS non-cash loss, is $10,866m (up 7%). Both values are correct on their own basis. This report compares continuing operations to continuing operations.

## 3. Driver attribution

### Underlying drivers

**Driver 1 — Net interest income: +$1,563m. Confidence 97.**
NII rose 7% to $25,586m. Volume did the work. Average interest earning assets rose $92bn (8%) to $1,246bn; average lending assets rose $60bn (7%) across home ($32bn), business and corporate ($27bn) and consumer lending (PA26, p28). The net interest margin fell 3bpts to 2.05%, but the bank states the margin was stable when it excludes liquid-asset growth (PA26, p28). Margin parts (PA26, p28-29): asset pricing -5bpts from competition (home -4, business and institutional -1); liquids -2bpts; Treasury and Markets -2bpts; portfolio mix +2bpts; capital, replicating and other +5bpts (replicating portfolio +4, capital hedges +1); funding costs and basis risk flat. RP26 (p25) confirms the +$1,563m block in the income bridge.

**Driver 2 — Other operating income: +$196m. Confidence 96.**
Other operating income rose 4% to $4,638m (PA26, p30). Parts: commissions +$135m (higher insurance income with the CommInsure General Insurance milestone payment, higher equities income, higher syndication fees; lower deposit, card and FX income); other income +$46m (minority investments and a fair value gain on the Gemini investment after its IPO; lower Structured Asset Finance revenue after the aircraft lease portfolio sale); lending fees +$12m; funds management +$12m; trading -$9m. The one-off gains sit in this driver. The bank does not quantify them one by one (PA26, p26 footnote 3).

**Driver 3 — Underlying operating expenses: -$719m. Confidence 97.**
Underlying expenses rose 6% to $13,585m. The bank's own bridge (PA26, p31; RP26, p27): inflation +$455m, investment in technology +$444m, investment in frontline and operations +$128m, other +$96m, productivity -$404m. By category: IT services +$393m (cloud volumes, software licensing, amortisation, infrastructure, resilience and AI), staff +$288m (wage inflation; average FTE +1,760 to 51,756), other +$57m, occupancy -$19m. Favourable FX gave a partial, unquantified offset.

**Driver 4 — Loan impairment expense: -$62m. Confidence 98.**
The expense rose 9% to $788m; the loan loss rate rose 1bpt to 8bpts of average GLAA (PA26, p34). Higher collective provisions reflect portfolio growth, geopolitical risk and macroeconomic uncertainty. Divisional parts (PA26, p34): Retail Banking Services +$106m, New Zealand +$11m, Corporate Centre +$6m, Business Banking -$45m (write-backs and lower individually assessed charges), Institutional Banking and Markets -$16m. These parts sum to +$62m.

**Driver 5 — Corporate tax expense: -$208m. Confidence 97.**
Tax rose 5% to $4,699m on higher pre-tax profit. The effective tax rate fell 50bpts to 30.0% because non-deductible expenses fell (PA26, p35). The lower rate held tax growth (5%) below pre-tax profit growth (6%).

### Notable and restructuring items: -$40m incremental. Confidence 95.

These items sit inside operating expenses and rose from $130m (FY25) to $170m (FY26) (PA26, p27). FY26 items: provisions for the settlement of NZ legal proceedings (the ASB Credit Contracts and Consumer Finance Act 2003 class action; PA26, p79); an additional goodwill payment after ASIC's Better Banking review; and domestic customer remediation. The bank does not split the $170m. FY25 items are split in PA25 (p27): domestic remediation $52m, NZ remediation $33m, Bankwest restructuring $45m.

### Divisional view (cash NPAT, continuing operations; PA26, p16)

Business Banking +$452m (to $4,544m, +11%), Retail Banking Services +$257m (to $5,587m, +5%), Institutional Banking and Markets +$20m (to $1,258m, +2%), Corporate Centre +$84m (loss narrowed to -$1,519m), New Zealand -$83m (to $1,112m, -7%; ASB carried the class-action settlement and higher impairment).

## 4. Disagreements between sources

1. **Loan impairment growth rate.** PA26 (p16, p34) says 9%; RP26 (p24) says 8.5%. The dollar values agree ($726m to $788m, +8.54%). This is rounding. Use the dollar values.
2. **Headline statutory NPAT.** ASX26 (p1) uses the continuing-operations value ($10,911m, +8%); the total statutory value is $10,866m (+7%) (PA26, p16). Use the continuing pair against cash NPAT; the bank headlines both measures on that basis.
3. **FY25 divisional numbers.** PA25 (p16) originally reported RBS $5,395m, IB&M $1,224m, NZ $1,198m, CC&O -$1,657m for FY25. PA26 (p16) restates these to $5,330m, $1,238m, $1,195m, -$1,603m. The group total is unchanged. Use the PA26 restated values for like-for-like comparison, because PA26 states comparatives "restated to conform with presentation in the current period".

## 5. Limitations

- The $170m FY26 notable items have no disclosed component split.
- The CommInsure milestone payment and the Gemini fair value gain are not quantified individually.
- The FX benefit inside expenses is not quantified.
- The bank discloses NIM drivers in basis points, not dollars; a dollar volume/margin split of the NII movement is not published.
- Figures come from text extraction of the PDFs; chart-only values were used only where tables or text repeat them.
