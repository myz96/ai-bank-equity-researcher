# CBA FY26 Net Interest Margin — Movement and Attribution

**Coverage:** Commonwealth Bank of Australia (ASX: CBA), Group level
**Period:** FY26 (year ended 30 June 2026) versus FY25 (year ended 30 June 2025)
**Basis:** Cash basis, continuing operations. NIM = net interest income / average interest earning assets, with mortgage offset balances netted down.
**Sources:** FY26 Profit Announcement (PA), FY26 Results Presentation (Pres), FY26 ASX Announcement (ASX), FY25 Profit Announcement. Page references are PDF pages.

---

## 1. Headline movement

Group NIM decreased 3 basis points, from 2.08% in FY25 to 2.05% in FY26.

- "Net interest margin (%) 2.05 | 2.08 | (3)bpts" (FY26 PA, p.18, Group KPI table; also p.28). The table is "Presented on a cash basis unless stated otherwise" (FY26 PA, p.18).
- "The Bank's net interest margin decreased by 3 basis points on the prior year to 2.05%." (FY26 PA, p.28)
- "Net interest margin 2.05% — 3bpts on FY25" (FY26 ASX, p.2)
- The comparator is confirmed at source: "a 9 basis point increase in net interest margin to 2.08%" (FY25 PA, p.28). The FY26 PA restates comparatives to current presentation (FY26 PA, p.11); the restated 2.08% equals the originally published value, so the comparator is clean.

Second-half momentum was positive: 2H26 NIM was 2.06%, up 2 bps on 1H26 (2.04%) (FY26 PA, p.28-29).

## 3. Where the sources differ

1. **Repo classification.** The PA waterfall shows Liquids -3 and Treasury & Markets -2 (repos inside T&M). The Pres shows Liquids & repos -4 (liquids -3, repos -1) and T&M -1. The combined -5 bps and the -3 bps total agree. I prefer the Pres split. It isolates the earnings-neutral repo balance effect from real Treasury income performance, and it matches the bank's own underlying narrative: "Margins were broadly stable excluding growth in liquid assets and institutional reverse sale and repurchase agreements, which have broadly neutral impacts on net interest income" (PA p.9; same text in ASX p.2).
2. **Home lending -4 bps split.** The PA labels the full -4 bps as pricing, "primarily reflecting the impact of competition" (p.28). The Pres splits it: competition -3, cash rate lag -1 (p.60). No numeric conflict; I prefer the Pres granularity.
3. **"Underlying" NIM.** The PA says underlying NIM was "stable" excluding liquids (p.28). The Pres shows underlying NIM up 1 bp, from a rebased FY25 of 2.04% to 2.05% (slide 6, p.6). The rebase "excludes 4 basis point mix impact from current period growth in average liquid assets and institutional repo balances" (Pres p.130, slide 6 note 4). The two statements describe the same result at different rounding.

## 4. Interpretation

The whole 3 bps decline is balance-sheet mix, not core margin. Liquids and repos grew and cut the ratio by 4 bps with minimal profit impact; the Pres slide title states this: "Lower margin largely due to growth in liquids and repos - competition offset by hedge earnings" (p.60). Inside the underlying margin, two forces offset: lending competition took 5 bps out (mostly home loans), while the rising rate environment returned 5 bps through the replicating portfolio and capital hedges (deposit hedge tractor 3.30% average, 3.58% exit; equity hedge 3.82% average, 3.91% exit - Pres p.61). The exit tractor rates sit above the FY26 averages, so the hedge tailwind carries into FY27. Portfolio mix added 2 bps because business lending and deposits grew faster than total assets. Sensitivity guides from the bank: each extra $10bn of liquids costs ~2 bps of Group NIM (Pres p.61).

A cross-check from the appendix: interest spread rose 6 bps to 1.52% while the benefit of interest-free liabilities fell 9 bps to 0.53% (FY26 PA, p.100). This is a different decomposition of the same -3 bps; the documents do not reconcile it to the driver waterfall, and I did not force a reconciliation.

## 5. Limitations

- The waterfall bps values come from chart data labels in extracted PDF text. The extraction scrambled label order; I verified each value against the narrative text and the arithmetic sum.
- The bank rounds each driver to whole bps; unrounded components may not sum exactly.
- Sub-splits sourced only from the Pres (repos -1; home competition -3 vs cash rate lag -1) lack a corroborating PA number.
- This report attributes the Group NIM only; divisional NIMs (RBS, Business Banking, IB&M, ASB) are out of scope.
