## 3. Most recent results (1H26): NIM walk, earnings bridge, headline measure

### NIM movement walk — two places

**Investor Discussion Pack, presentation section, slide 24** ("NET INTEREST MARGIN (%)"): waterfall from 1H25 (1.92%) and 2H25 (1.95%) to 1H26 (1.89%), split into Timing difference, Loans, Deposits, Liquid assets, Capital & other, Wholesale funding, Treasury & Markets. The slide also gives Core NIM (1.78%) vs Treasury & Markets contribution (0.11%), 2H26 considerations, and rate sensitivities. Adjacent slide 23 walks operating income (Core NIM movement, balance sheet growth, volatile items, fees, Markets).

**Results Announcement, "Review of earnings" section, printed page 7** (PDF page 13): "Net interest margin movement" — two waterfall charts (1H26 vs 2H25 and 1H26 vs 1H25, excluding Notable Items) with bullet commentary per driver (loan spread, deposit spread, timing difference, liquid assets, wholesale funding, capital and other, T&M). This is the narrative version with basis-point attribution.

Files: wbc-1H26-presentation-IDP-2026.pdf and wbc-1H26-financial-results-announcement-2026.pdf (URLs in section 1).

### Earnings bridge

**Investor Discussion Pack, "Earnings drivers" section (slides 36-48):**
- Slide 37 "1H26 NET PROFIT": waterfall 2H25 ($3,515m) to 1H26 ($3,483m) across Net interest income, Non-interest income, Expenses, Impairment charges, Tax & NCI — on the ex-Notable-Items basis.
- Slide 38 "1H26 NET PROFIT": same waterfall vs 1H25 ($3,457m to $3,483m).
- Slide 39 "NET PROFIT EXCLUDING NOTABLE ITEMS AND NET PROFIT RECONCILIATION": table reconciling statutory net profit (1H26 $3,414m) to net profit ex Notable Items ($3,483m), with the Notable Items split (large items, hedging items) and EPS on both bases.

**Results Announcement:** the narrative bridge sits in "Review of earnings" (printed pages 6-15), line item by line item (net interest income, non-interest income, expenses, impairment charges), after "Statutory results" (printed page 3), "Notable Items" (printed page 4), "Performance summary" (printed page 4) and "Financial information" (printed page 5). There is no waterfall chart for profit in the Results Announcement; the waterfalls live in the IDP.

### Headline profit measure

Westpac headlines **net profit** on two bases side by side: **statutory net profit** first, then **"Net profit excluding Notable Items"**. The 1H26 ASX announcement highlights box (Results Announcement, PDF page 4) leads with "$3.4bn Statutory net profit" next to "$3.5bn Net profit ex Notable Items". The IDP scorecard (slide 8) headlines the ex-Notables basis (net profit ex Notable Items, ROTE ex Notable Items, cost-to-income ex Notable Items) with a footnote pointing to the statutory reconciliation on slide 39.

"Cash earnings" is gone. Timeline, verified from primary documents:
- FY22 result (media release, 7 November 2022) still headlined both: "Statutory net profit $5,694m" and "Cash earnings $5,276m" ( https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/aw/ic/FY22_Media_Release.pdf ).
- "1H23 Reporting Changes Update", 24 March 2023: "Net profit is the single measure the Group will use to assess overall financial performance… Report net profit only… Cash earnings specific sections removed." It also announced that the results announcement and annual report would combine ( https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/aw/ic/1H23_reporting_changes.pdf ).
- So cash earnings was last used at FY22 (November 2022); from 1H23 (May 2023) the headline is net profit, with Notable Items disclosed separately.
- The FY25 Results Announcement (page 2) describes "Income statement measures excluding Notable Items" as "comparable to a 'cash earnings' basis" ( https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/aw/ic/wbc-full-year-financial-results-2025.pdf ).

## 4. Format drift: FY20/FY21 vs FY25/1H26

Suites compared from the year landing pages (2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2025, current-year — URLs in section 1) and the downloaded documents.

**FY20 suite (2 November 2020)** — source: https://www.westpac.com.au/about-westpac/investor-centre/events-and-presentations/2020-presentations-and-agm/
- 2020 Full Year Financial Results Media Release: https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/aw/ic/Westpac_2020_media_release.pdf
- 2020 Full Year Financial Results Announcement (the full ASX profit announcement): https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/aw/ic/ASX_Full_Year_Results_Announcement_2020.pdf
- Full Year Financial Results Presentation and Investor Discussion Pack: https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/aw/ic/WBC_FY20_Presentation_and_IDP.pdf
- No Excel workbook. No separate Risk Factors document listed. Pillar 3 published on the regulatory disclosures page.

**FY21 suite (1 November 2021)** — source: https://www.westpac.com.au/about-westpac/investor-centre/events-and-presentations/2021-presentations-and-agm/ — same four-document shape (media release, ASX_Full_Year_Financial_Results_2021.pdf, FY21_Presentation_and_IDP.pdf, plus a "notables" pre-release WBC_FY21_Notables.pdf). Quarterly events in 2020-2021 were "Capital, Funding and Asset Quality" update slide packs plus Pillar 3, not full quarterly IDPs.

**What changed between then and now:**

1. **Headline measure.** FY20/FY21 headlined cash earnings alongside statutory profit. Since 1H23 the headline is net profit (statutory plus ex-Notable-Items view). Source: 1H23 Reporting Changes Update.
2. **Excel arrived.** The Key Financial Information workbook first appeared at 1H23. FY20-FY22 events have no machine-readable pack.
3. **Full-year announcement shrank; Annual Report absorbed the financial report.** The FY20/FY21 ASX results announcements were large standalone documents with the financial statements inside. The 1H23 changes combined the results announcement and the annual report. At FY23 the suite listed the Annual Report plus a short "Second Half Financial Performance Review" ( https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/aw/ic/second-half-financial-performance-review.pdf ). At FY25 a "Full Year Financial Results Announcement" title returned, but as a 67-page performance review (Group performance, Segment reporting, Other information) with no financial statements; the audited financial report is in the Annual Report. The half-year announcement still contains the Interim Financial Report (1H26: printed pages 50-88) because Appendix 4D requires it.
4. **Quarterly disclosure got richer.** 2020-2021: "Capital, Funding and Asset Quality" slide packs. Since about 2023 (verified for 3Q23 onward): a written quarterly "Update" ASX release plus a quarterly Investor Discussion Pack, alongside Pillar 3.
5. **Pillar 3 became machine-readable and moved into the results suite.** From 1 January 2025, APRA's revised APS 330 prescribes formats; Westpac now publishes a quarterly Pillar 3 quantitative Excel and lists Pillar 3 documents inside each results event.
6. **New satellite documents.** Recent events add "Risk Factors" (1H26) and pre-announcement "Items Impacting … Results" releases (the FY20-era analogue was "Items affecting" / "notables" ASX releases, so this is continuity with a name change: "Factors Affecting 2H21 Results" at FY21, "Notable Items" at FY22-FY23, "Notable Items and Reporting Changes" at 1H25, "Items Impacting" at FY25/1H26).
7. **Return metric.** ROTE (return on average tangible equity) now appears beside ROE and leads the IDP scorecard (1H26 slide 8 headlines "ROTE ex Notable Items"). FY20/FY21 packs headlined ROE on a cash-earnings basis. UNVERIFIED: the exact period when ROTE first entered the suite (it is present in FY25 and 1H26 documents; the 30 April 2025 reporting-changes release does not mention it).
8. **Segment names changed** across the period (e.g. current segments: Consumer, Business & Wealth, Institutional (labelled Westpac Institutional Bank in some tables), New Zealand, Group Businesses — per the 1H26 KFI workbook and 1H25 reporting-changes appendix).

Practical consequence for a parser: time-series continuity breaks at 1H23 (cash earnings to net profit; document set redefined) and again at 1H25 (Pillar 3 format change; APS 330). Filename conventions also shifted around 2024-2025 to a "wbc-…" prefix.

## 5. Headline profit vs statutory net profit: presentation and definitions

- **Statutory net profit** (net profit attributable to owners of WBC): defined by Australian Accounting Standards; audited/reviewed. In the 1H26 Results Announcement it leads: Appendix 4D "Results for announcement to the market" (page iii), the ASX announcement highlights (PDF page 4), and "Group performance — Statutory results" (printed page 3). The Interim Financial Report (printed pages 50-88) holds the reviewed statements. For full-year, the audited statements sit in the Annual Report ( https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/aw/ic/wbc-annual-report-2025.pdf ).
- **Net profit excluding Notable Items**: a non-AAS measure "used by Westpac for internal management reporting, as it provides a clearer view of the Group's underlying operational performance… not defined under Australian Accounting Standards, nor is it audited or reviewed" (1H26 IDP slide 39, same wording in the Results Announcement).
- **Where each is defined:** the Results Announcement "Additional Information" section contains "Non-AAS financial measures" (1H26: printed pages ~92-97; a table gives MEASURE/RATIO, DESCRIPTION, FURTHER INFORMATION for each measure, e.g. Core NIM, adjusted dividend payout ratio) and a "Glossary of Abbreviations and Defined Terms" (printed page 104). The "Notable Items" subsection (printed page 4) itemises the adjustments (currently two classes: large items and hedging items — per IDP slide 39).
- **Reconciliation locations:** IDP slide 39 (statutory 3,414 / Notable Items (69) / ex-Notables 3,483 for 1H26, with EPS both ways); Results Announcement "Impact of Notable Items" (KFI sheet 1.4 mirrors it); "Financial information" table (printed page 5) shows every shareholder metric on both bases.
- **Which one Westpac pushes:** media release and ASX highlights give statutory first and ex-Notables second; the IDP scorecard and all IDP performance slides use ex-Notables (footnoted). Segment reporting is on a net profit basis including Notable Items (per the 1H23 Reporting Changes Update, slide "2.0 Segment reporting").
