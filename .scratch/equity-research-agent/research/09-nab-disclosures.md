# NAB disclosure inventory

Research date: 2026-08-25. All findings come from primary sources: nab.com.au shareholder centre pages and the published PDF/XLSX documents. I downloaded and read the key PDFs. Page references use two systems: "PDF p" is the physical PDF page, "printed p" is the page number printed in the document footer.

Reporting rhythm: the financial year ends 30 September. NAB reports the full year in early November and the half year in early May. NAB also gives a first-quarter trading update in February and a third-quarter trading update in August. The most recent results event is 1H26, reported Monday 4 May 2026. The most recent disclosure event is the 3Q26 trading update, Monday 17 August 2026.

## 4. Format drift: FY20/FY21 vs today

I compared the FY20 Full Year Results Announcement (120-page PDF, TOC extracted), the FY21 MD&A (88 pages), and the current documents.

1. **The statutory financial report left the full-year results book at FY21.** FY20's "Full Year Results Announcement" contained Section 3 Financial Report (income statement, balance sheet, notes, compliance statement) and ASX Appendix 4E. From FY21, the full-year results document became "Full Year Results – Management Discussion and Analysis" with no financial-report section; the audited financial report moved to the annual reporting suite, published the same day (the FY21 Annual Financial Report is signed 9 November 2021 — the results date; verified inside https://www.nab.com.au/content/dam/nab/documents/reports/corporate/2021-annual-financial-report.pdf, directors' authorisation on PDF p95). **Half-year books still contain the reviewed interim financial report and Appendix 4D** (1H26: Appendix 4D at PDF p3, Section 4 Financial report at PDF pp56–91).
2. **Annual-report packaging changed at FY22.** FY16–FY21: separate "Annual Review" + "Annual Financial Report" (FY21 URLs verified: `2021-annual-review.pdf`, `2021-annual-financial-report.pdf`). From FY22: one combined "Annual Report" (`2022-annual-report.pdf`, `2024-annual-report.pdf`, `2025-annual-report.pdf` all HTTP 200).
3. **The KPM Excel workbook stopped after FY20** (see section 2 above).
4. **Section structure of the results book changed at FY21.** FY20: 1 Group Review, 2 Divisional Review, 3 Financial Report, 4 Supplementary Information, 5 Glossary. FY21 onward: Section 1 Group highlights, Section 2 Group review, Section 3 Divisional review, Section 4 Supplementary information (FY) or Section 4 Financial report + Section 5 Supplementary information (HY), then Glossary. The cash-earnings reconciliation stayed in Supplementary information throughout (FY20: item 9, printed pp109–110; FY21: item 15; 1H26: printed pp100–101).
5. **Pillar 3 joined the results-page suite around FY23.** The FY20, FY21, and FY22 rows on the financial results page list no Pillar 3 report; FY23 onward rows include it. The Pillar 3 XLSX appeared in 2025, and the quarterly "Supplementary Disclosures" document appears in the 3Q26 row.
6. **Large notable items treatment moved into the headline presentation.** FY21 introduced a dedicated "Large notable items" supplementary note (item 16). In 1H26 NAB again presents "cash earnings excluding large notable items" as a headline measure (the $1,347m pre-tax / $949m post-tax accelerated software amortisation charge; Section 1, printed p14).
7. **Asset path drift:** pre-2021 documents live under `/content/dam/nabrwd/...`; 2021 onward under `/content/dam/nab/...`.

Continuity: the cash-earnings framework, the NIM movement chart in Section 2, the divisional cash-earnings split, and the average balance sheet appear in every period examined (FY20, FY21, FY25, 1H26). Time-series extraction is feasible across the drift.

## 5. Cash earnings vs statutory net profit

- **Primacy:** NAB presents Group results on a cash earnings basis unless it states otherwise. Cash earnings drives internal management reporting and divisional reporting. Source: 1H26 Half Year Results, printed p12 (PDF p16), and Investor Presentation slide 39.
- **Definition location:** "Information about cash earnings and other non-IFRS measures", the first pages of Section 1 Group highlights — 1H26: printed p12 (PDF p16); FY25 MD&A: Section 1 (PDF p12 area). The Glossary at the back of each results book defines cash earnings, cash ROE, and related terms.
- **Definition substance:** cash earnings = statutory net profit from continuing operations, adjusted for non-cash earnings items. 1H26 exclusions: (a) hedging and fair value volatility, (b) amortisation of acquired intangible assets, (c) acquisitions, integration, disposals and business closures. Cash earnings is not audited and is not an IFRS measure; the auditor (EY) reviews the statutory statements in Section 4.
- **Reconciliation locations (1H26):** summary bridge in the Group performance results table, printed p13 (PDF p17); full line-by-line tables printed pp100–101 (PDF pp104–105); non-cash item descriptions printed p99. FY25 MD&A: supplementary item 16, printed pp73–74 (PDF pp75–76). FY20: supplementary item 9. The structure of this reconciliation is stable since at least FY20.
- **Statutory net profit** appears in the same Group performance results table (bottom rows, including discontinued operations), in the Section 4 income statement (half-year books), and in the Annual Report (full year).
- **Large notable items** form a second, separate adjustment layer: they stay inside cash earnings but NAB also shows "cash earnings excluding large notable items" (1H26: $2,639m cash earnings; $3,588m excluding the $949m post-tax software amortisation LNI).

## 7. UNVERIFIED / caveats

- **ASX lodgement list:** the ASX announcements page renders through JavaScript; I could not read the lodgement titles or confirm the exact set of files lodged with the ASX on each results day. Cross-check on asx.com.au (ticker NAB) if lodgement-level fidelity matters.
- **FY20 Annual Financial Report publication date:** the annual reporting suite page shows no dates. I did not verify whether the FY20 AFR came out on results day or later. (FY21's same-day publication is verified via the signature date.)
- **Start date of the quarterly "Supplementary Disclosures" document:** verified present for 3Q26; I did not check earlier quarters.
- **Pillar 3 XLSX before 2025:** the regulatory disclosures page describes the XLSX as available "from 2025 onwards"; I verified 2025–2026 files only and did not probe for older ones.
- **Results call transcripts:** the results rows link webcast replays only. The investor briefings page shows transcripts for some non-results events (for example the July 2026 Business Banking session), and the 1H26 news release references a media-call transcript. I did not confirm a systematic transcript archive for half/full-year calls.
- **Excel packs between FY21 and FY24:** the financial results page lists none; absence from the listing is my only evidence that none were published.
- **News-release figure discrepancy:** a summarised fetch of the 1H26 news release reported cash earnings ex-LNI of "$3,558m"; the results book states $3,588m (printed p13). Trust the results book.
