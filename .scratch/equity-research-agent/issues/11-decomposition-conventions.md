# 11 — Sell-side decomposition conventions per metric

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

For each of the six headline metrics, what does a first-pass analyst note conventionally attribute movement to, and with what vocabulary? Ground the answer in high-trust sources: the banks' own results materials and glossaries, APRA definitions and capital framework documents, and RBA publications on bank margins and profitability. Output: a per-metric list of conventional drivers, the standard decomposition arithmetic where one exists, and terminology to adopt or avoid.

## Answer

Full findings, with citations: [research/11-decomposition-conventions.md](../research/11-decomposition-conventions.md). Grounded in four primary results PDFs (NAB FY25, Westpac FY25, CBA FY25, ANZ 1H26), APS 110–117, APG 110, AASB 9, and RBA publications; walk labels quoted verbatim.

1. **NIM walks share a stable driver set across all four banks**, expressed in basis points: lending margin, deposits, wholesale funding, liquid assets, capital and replicating portfolio, mix, and a Markets/Treasury bar. Labels differ per bank. "Ex-volatile" NIM is not one concept: NAB "ex M&T", Westpac "Core NIM", ANZ "excl. Markets", CBA "excl. liquids and pooled facilities".
2. **Core-profit vocabulary diverges the most:** NAB "cash earnings", ANZ "cash profit", CBA "cash NPAT", Westpac "net profit excluding Notable Items" (no cash measure). ANZ says "significant items", not "notable items".
3. **The CET1 walk convention:** cash earnings, dividend net of DRP, RWA by type (credit, market, operational, IRRBB), model and regulatory changes, capital floor adjustment, buybacks, divestments. IRRBB RWA is a separate, volatile bar at all four banks (APS 117).
4. **Impairment vocabulary differs:** "credit impairment charge" (NAB/ANZ) vs "impairment charges" (WBC) vs "loan impairment expense" (CBA); collective vs individual provisions plus forward-looking overlays; loss rate in bps of gross loans with bank-specific denominators.
5. **"Jaws"** is defined formally only by CBA; the others use it informally.

Five small points are flagged UNVERIFIED in the research file. One of them — Westpac's cash-earnings retirement date — is already resolved by ticket 10: the "1H23 Reporting Changes Update" of 24 March 2023.
