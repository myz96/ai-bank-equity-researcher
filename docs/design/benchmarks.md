# Frontier benchmarks (standing quality target)

Per ADR-0004: fresh Fable agents, zero project context, same cached documents,
no taxonomy given. These outputs (out/baseline-fable/) are the ceiling the
pipeline optimises toward; rerun them when the pipeline is complete. The tier
above — a human sector expert scoring both — remains on the map as fog.

## Readings

1. **Walk metrics: the pipeline is near frontier at ~1/1000th the cost.**
   The remaining recall gap is mostly unclaimed zero-value bars.
2. **Bridge metrics: the frontier gap is concentrated and specified.** The
   benchmark's working names the exact evidence the pipeline misses: FY25
   comparative levels (tax, notables), the slide-25 income waterfall, and the
   headline-vs-underlying expense framing. That is ticket 25's spec.
3. **Calibration convergence from two directions.** Fable self-reports 93–96
   and earns it; the pipeline earns its numbers through structural caps
   (claims at 95+ have been 100% correct on the last two scorecards).
4. **Format drift is not the moat we feared** — both systems handle FY21; the
   robustness risk concentrates in disclosure *shape* (bridge vs walk), not
   document age.
