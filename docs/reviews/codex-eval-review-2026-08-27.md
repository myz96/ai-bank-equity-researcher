## Must fix before claiming results

1. **Incomplete gold is silently converted into false labels, corrupting precision and calibration.** `_gold_driver_values` retains only entries with numeric `value`; once any numeric gold exists, every unmatched model claim is labelled wrong rather than unknown ([evals.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/evals.py:138), [evals.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/evals.py:181)). For FY21 cash earnings, `nii` is explicitly “not probed,” yet NII, other income, and tax claims are counted false ([cba-fy21.json](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/gold/cba-fy21.json:44), [raw score](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/results/20260827-0801-cheap-dev.jsonl:8)). Conversely, when no numeric gold exists, all claims disappear from calibration.  
   Remedy: Represent labels as correct/incorrect/unscored and calculate precision and Brier only over independently verified, exhaustive claim slots.

2. **The reported calibration covers a narrow survivorship-biased slice, not the agent or even the whole attribution.** The 36 claims in the later report come from only six CBA cases—28 NIM claims and eight cash-earnings claims; crashes, abstentions, movement errors, arithmetic tiers, impairment claims, and whole-attribution confidence are excluded ([scorecard](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/results/20260827-0801-cheap-dev.md:25), [evals.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/evals.py:214)). The report contains movement failures at confidence 95 and 100 that have no effect on Brier ([scorecard](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/results/20260827-0801-cheap-dev.md:7)). This contradicts the intent to calibrate both driver and whole-attribution confidence, per metric and evidence layer ([confidence intent](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/.scratch/equity-research-agent/issues/02-confidence-and-calibration.md:15)).  
   Remedy: Report coverage and failure rates first, then separate movement, driver, narrative, and whole-attribution calibration over predefined evaluable populations.

3. **There is no defensible performance holdout, and development has explicitly optimized against the scored cases.** All 23 metric cases remain `dev`; the planned roughly 34/8 metric split has not happened, and the two `holdout_candidate` cases are still executed as dev ([gold README](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/gold/README.md:22), [cba-fy21.json](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/gold/cba-fy21.json:9)). Ticket 25 explicitly changes retrieval and prompts to reach 4/4 on CBA FY26 cash earnings, then verifies against the same harness ([ticket 25](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/.scratch/equity-research-agent/issues/25-cash-earnings-bridge-iteration.md:8)). I found no direct runtime flow of gold values into the prompt—`run_suite` passes case metadata only—but the experimental leakage is substantial ([evals.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/evals.py:239), [author.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/author.py:116)).  
   Remedy: Freeze a balanced metric holdout now, keep it outside the developer-visible workspace, and have an independent person own its gold and execution.

4. **Driver precision/recall has inconsistent alternate-framing and hierarchy semantics.** Each claim may independently match any framing, allowing an impossible hybrid of values from different source decompositions to score fully correct; recall is nevertheless always calculated against the primary framing’s keys ([evals.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/evals.py:169)). A completely correct alternate framing can therefore lose recall, while an incoherent mixture can gain precision. Exact canonical matching also marks `funding.deposits` wrong against a `funding` parent, as already happened for FY21 ([raw score](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/results/20260826-0634-cheap-dev.jsonl:2)); the CET1 gold even uses an `rwa` parent absent from the taxonomy ([cba-fy26.json](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/gold/cba-fy26.json:91), [taxonomy.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/taxonomy.py:135)). Duplicate canonical claims are also allowed and can inflate precision and calibration sample size.  
   Remedy: Enforce unique claims and score the answer one-to-one against one coherent eligible framing, with explicit parent/child aggregation rules.

5. **The extraction metric can award full credit to a materially wrong extraction.** It ignores labels, units, source, comparison, and bar identity; each gold value merely needs to appear anywhere among any walk record’s numbers, and the same extracted number can satisfy multiple gold bars ([evals.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/evals.py:199)). Thus one extracted zero can satisfy several zero bars, permuted labels score perfectly, and endpoints or unrelated walks can create hits. It even reports 4/4 extraction for the FY26 CET1 HoH walk while driver scoring correctly says that walk is not FY-on-FY ([scorecard](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/results/20260827-0801-cheap-dev.md:21), [cba-fy26.json](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/gold/cba-fy26.json:93)).  
   Remedy: Match bars one-to-one by normalized label/canonical ID, value, unit, source framing, and endpoint comparison.

6. **Movement scoring does not verify the answer’s unit, basis, or comparator, and its tolerances disagree with validation.** `score_case` derives the unit from gold and compares only three numbers; an answer with the wrong unit or cash/statutory/ex-notables basis can score OK ([evals.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/evals.py:158)). The harness uses a flat ±$10m, while the documented rule is ±max(1%, $10m); validation uses ±0.51 for movement arithmetic regardless of unit and separate 1/10-unit reconciliation tolerances ([evals.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/evals.py:17), [validate.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/validate.py:18), [validate.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/validate.py:51)). This can make the same answer valid in one layer and invalid in another.  
   Remedy: Use one typed tolerance implementation and require exact metric, basis, period/comparator, and contribution-unit agreement before numeric matching.

7. **The cross-reference “holdout” measures page citation, not answer correctness.** `score_crossref` awards coverage whenever a required page is cited by a key fact; it does not verify that the answer states the required fact or that the citation entails it ([evals.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/evals.py:57)). Fact checking is explicitly a stub ([evals.py](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/src/bank_equity_researcher/evals.py:82)). The mortgage-offset result received 1/1 despite omitting required half-year balances and adding an unsupported causal interpretation about “inflating” NIM ([crossref result](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/results/20260827-0722-cheap-crossref.jsonl:2)).  
   Remedy: Score retrieval coverage and claim-level factual/entailment accuracy separately, with independent judges or human adjudication before treating these as holdout passes.

8. **The gold tier policy is stronger than the gold and harness actually implement.** The README promises arithmetic identity checks, citation-graded narrative drivers, and honest residual handling ([gold README](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/gold/README.md:8)); the harness ignores `direction`, `value_pct`, narrative checklists, residual truth, and most impairment detail. Specific gold ambiguities include FY26 underlying expenses versus the headline/notable-items split ([cba-fy26.json](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/gold/cba-fy26.json:47)), a HoH CET1 walk attached to an FY-on-FY case ([cba-fy26.json](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/gold/cba-fy26.json:91)), “not probed” FY21 components, and a crossref `$130m` comparator inferred from a benchmark rather than directly sighted in primary disclosure ([cba-fy26-crossref.json](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/gold/cba-fy26-crossref.json:40)).  
   Remedy: Give every claim a structured provenance and evaluation status, independently re-derive the scored gold, and quarantine inferred or ambiguous facts until adjudicated.

9. **The calibration statistics are descriptive noise at this size and dependence structure.** In the later run the headline rate is 9 wrong out of 34 claims at ≥85, or 26.5%; even treating claims as independent gives an approximate 95% Wilson interval of 14.6%–43.1%. The earlier run was 2/24, or 8.3%, with an interval of roughly 2.3%–25.8% ([first scorecard](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/results/20260826-0634-cheap-dev.md:15), [second scorecard](/Users/michaelzhao/swe/ai-bank-equity-researcher/evals/results/20260827-0801-cheap-dev.md:25)). Claims are clustered within six cases and shared walks, so those intervals are optimistic. Brier 0.229 is likewise only a point estimate on a changing, partly mislabelled slice—not evidence that the system is calibrated.  
   Remedy: State raw numerators, case count, metric mix, coverage, uncertainty, and “descriptive for this run only”; use repeated runs and case-cluster bootstrap intervals before external claims.
